## 1. Stanley Kubrick — 秩序中的恐惧

### 1.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | Steadicam稳定滑行+缓慢追踪+对称轨道 | Steadicam glide, slow tracking, symmetric dolly, Kubrick-style |
| 构图 | 严格单点透视+中心放置+广角纵深=秩序不可逃 | strict one-point perspective, centered, wide angle depth, order is inescapable, Kubrick-grade |
| 调色 | 冷色为主+红/橙点缀=暴力在秩序中 | cold dominant, red/orange accent, violence within order, Kubrick-palette |
| 光线 | 均匀照明(恐怖)+无阴影=无处可藏 | even illumination (horror), no shadows, nowhere to hide, Kubrick-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 跳切(2001太空)+长镜头(闪灵)=节奏控制 | jump cut (2001), long take (Shining), rhythm control |
| 叙事 | 理性即疯狂+秩序即暴力+系统碾压个体 | ationality as madness, order as violence, system crushes individual |

### 1.2 拉片精华

**The Shining (1980)** — DP: John Alcott
- Steadicam: Danny骑三轮车走廊=观众即追踪者
- 构图: 对称走廊+单点透视+极广=酒店即迷宫
- 光线: 均匀照明+几乎无阴影=恐怖不是黑暗，是无处可藏
- 色彩: 浴室红+走廊橙+雪白=暴力在日常生活色中
- AI适配: Steadicam tracking corridor, symmetric one-point perspective, even illumination, no shadows, horror is nowhere to hide

**2001: A Space Odyssey (1968)**:
- 跳切: 骨头→飞船=百万年一剪=时间即暴力
- AI适配: jump cut bone to spaceship, million years in one cut, time as violence

**A Clockwork Orange (1971)**:
- 广角畸变: 极广+中心=疯狂从中心扩散
- 色彩: 超饱和白+红+橙=暴力的狂欢色
- AI适配: ultra-wide distortion, centered, oversaturated white/red/orange, violence as carnival

## 2. Robert Eggers — 历史的窒息

### 2.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 锁定+缓慢横移+方形画幅+无手持 | locked-off, slow lateral, square aspect, no handheld, Eggers-style |
| 构图 | 方形画幅(1.19:1/1.66:1)+中心+压迫空间 | square aspect, centered, oppressive space, Eggers-grade |
| 调色 | 极度去饱和+黑白+灰棕+只有火是暖的 | extreme desaturation, near B&W, grey-brown, only fire is warm, Eggers-palette |
| 光线 | 纯自然光/蜡烛/火把=只有时代准确的光源 | only period-accurate light, candle/torch/fire, no modern fill, Eggers-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 极少剪辑+长镜头+节奏来自沉默 | minimal cuts, long takes, rhythm from silence |
| 叙事 | 历史=噩梦+宗教=疯狂+孤立=幻觉 | history as nightmare, religion as madness, isolation as hallucination |
| 美术 | 极致历史考据+手工制作+无现代痕迹 | extreme historical accuracy, handcrafted, no modern trace |

### 2.2 拉片精华

**The Witch (2015)** — DP: Jarin Blaschke
- 方形画幅: 1.66:1=压迫+无逃跑空间
- 光线: 只有自然光+蜡烛+火=17世纪只有这些
- 全片: 灰棕+去饱和+冷蓝底+只有火暖=新英格兰即地狱
- AI适配: 1.66:1 aspect, period-accurate light only, grey-brown desaturated, cold blue base, only fire is warm

**The Lighthouse (2019)** — DP: Jarin Blaschke
- 方形画幅: 1.19:1=极压迫+灯塔即牢笼
- 全片: 黑白+ nitrate负片质感+高对比=19世纪摄影
- 光线: 煤油灯+灯塔光=旋转的光=疯狂即旋转
- AI适配: 1.19:1 square, black and white, nitrate negative texture, kerosene lamp, rotating lighthouse beam

**The Northman (2022)** — DP: Jarin Blaschke
- 维京复仇: 自然光+血+泥+雪=暴力即元素
- 关键技法: 战斗用长镜头+不切=暴力的真实时间
- AI适配: 
atural light, blood mud snow, violence as elements, long take combat, no cuts, Eggers-Northman

| 西幻场景 | Eggers适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| 中世纪地牢 | 方形画幅+蜡烛唯一光源 | square aspect, candle only light source, period-accurate, dungeon, Eggers |

### 3.2 拉片精华

## 5. Nosferatu (2024) — Eggers的吸血鬼语法(帧级拉片)

> Robert Eggers + Jarin Blaschke + Linda Muir(服装) + Craig Lopatin(美术)
> 这是Eggers暗黑美学的集大成之作，对中世纪西幻暗黑场景有极高参考价值

### 5.1 六维度签名(Nosferatu专属)

| 维度 | 签名特征 | 英文提示词 | 情绪内核 |
|------|---------|-----------|---------|
| 构图 | 哥特拱门=框架/阴影=角色/负空间=恐惧 | gothic arch as frame, shadow as character, negative space = dread, Nosferatu-2024 | 哥特即恐惧 |
| 光线 | 只蜡烛+壁炉+月光/无现代填充/影子是活的 | candle+fireplace+moonlight only, no modern fill, shadow is alive, Nosferatu-2024-light | 光即恐惧的边界 |
| 调色 | 极去饱和+冷蓝底+暖蜡+偶尔血红 | extreme desat, cold blue base, warm candle, occasional blood red, Nosferatu-2024-grade | 血是唯一的颜色 |
| 运镜 | 缓慢横移/锁定/推入=恐惧从边缘来 | slow lateral, locked, push-in, dread approaches from edges, Nosferatu-2024 | 恐惧不急 |
| 剪辑 | 极少切/长持/影子动切/节奏=心跳 | minimal cuts, long holds, cut on shadow movement, rhythm = heartbeat, Nosferatu-2024-edit | 心跳=剪辑节奏 |
| 叙事 | 欲望=诅咒/疾病=隐喻/死亡=变形 | desire = curse, disease = metaphor, death = transformation, Nosferatu-2024-narrative | 吸血鬼是欲望的形状 |
