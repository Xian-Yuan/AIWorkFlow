## 1. Dante Ferretti — 历史的重构(Scorsese所有/Tim Burton/Passion of Christ/Terminator: Salvation)

### 1.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 建筑 | 巨大+精细+历史考据+镀金+腐烂=奢华即衰败 | massive, detailed, historical research, gilt, decay, opulence as decline, Ferretti-architecture |
| 道具 | 铁锈+蚀刻+手工+纹理=使用即痕迹 | rust, etching, handcrafted, texture, use as trace, Ferretti-props |
| 色彩 | 金+红+黑+蜡烛暖=历史即颜色 | gold, red, black, candle warm, history as color, Ferretti-color |
| 空间 | 垂直纵深+拱门+走廊+高穹顶=权力即高度 | vertical depth, arch, corridor, high dome, power as height, Ferretti-space |
| 纹理 | 石+铁+木+蜡+尘=时间即纹理 | stone, iron, wood, wax, dust, time as texture, Ferretti-texture |
| 世界观 | 每个建筑有历史=世界有过去=道具有故事 | every building has history, world has past, props have story, Ferretti-worldbuilding |

### 1.2 核心作品拉片

**Gangs of New York (2002)**: 五点区=贫穷+教堂=权力+铁+血+尘=纽约即战场
AI适配: `Five Points as poverty, church as power, iron+blood+dust, city as battlefield, Ferretti-Gangs`

**Sweeney Todd (2007)**: 伦敦暗+管道+雾+血+铁=城市即屠宰场
AI适配: `London dark, pipes, fog, blood, iron, city as slaughterhouse, Ferretti-Sweeney`

**The Age of Innocence (1993)**: 镀金+花+仪式+红+压抑=优雅即牢笼
AI适配: `gilt, flowers, ritual, red, repression, elegance as cage, Ferretti-Age`

### 1.3 AI短剧适配(中世纪西幻)

| 西幻场景 | Ferretti适配 | 提示词公式 |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| 王城/宫殿(衰败) | 巨大+镀金+腐烂+蜡烛暖+权力即高度 | `massive, gilt decay, candle warm, power as height, Ferretti-architecture, decaying palace` |
| 地牢/铁匠 | 铁锈+蚀刻+铁+蜡+尘=使用即痕迹 | `rust, etching, iron, wax, dust, use as trace, Ferretti-props, dungeon forge` |
| 仪式空间 | 高穹顶+拱门+金+红+蜡烛=权力即建筑 | `high dome, arch, gold+red, candle, power as architecture, Ferretti-ritual space` |
