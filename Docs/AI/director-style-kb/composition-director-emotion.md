## 5. Vertical 9:16 Composition Special (15 Rules)

> Douyin/TikTok vertical video has fundamentally different composition rules from 16:9

| # | Rule | Why | Horizontal Habit | Vertical Adaptation | AI Prompt |
|---|------|-----|-----------------|-------------------|----------|
| 1 | Vertical leading lines | Height = power | Horizontal leading lines to subject | Vertical lines (towers, trees, pillars) to subject | vertical leading lines, tower pillar tree, height=power, 9:16 |
| 2 | Top = future, bottom = past | Reading order | Left=past, right=future | Top=above/future, bottom=ground/past | top future bottom past, vertical reading order, 9:16 |
| 3 | Face at 1/3 from top | Eye-level comfort | Rule of thirds horizontal | Face at upper third, body fills below | face upper third, body fills frame below, 9:16 |
| 4 | Single subject dominant | Less horizontal space | Two-subject compositions | One subject + environment | single subject dominant, less horizontal space, 9:16 |
| 5 | Vertical split = hierarchy | Upstairs/downstairs | Left/right power split | Top/bottom power split | vertical split hierarchy, top powerful bottom weak, 9:16 |
| 6 | Full body = power stance | Standing = authority | Wide shot for context | Full body fills vertical frame | full body vertical, standing power stance, 9:16 |
| 7 | Looking up = larger | Vertical awe | Horizontal awe via wide | Low angle fills frame with subject | low angle vertical, subject fills frame, awe, 9:16 |
| 8 | Environment top+bottom | Frame with world | Side framing | Sky above + ground below | sky above ground below, vertical frame with world, 9:16 |
| 9 | Shallow DOF essential | Less clutter | Wide compositions hide clutter | Shallow DOF isolates subject | shallow DOF, isolate subject from background, 9:16 |
| 10 | Text space at bottom | Title/caption room | Text overlay anywhere | Bottom 20% reserved for text | bottom text space, title area reserved, 9:16 |
| 11 | Weapon vertical = threat | Sword pointing up | Sword horizontal = ready | Sword vertical = imminent | weapon vertical, sword pointing up imminent, 9:16 |
| 12 | Staircase = class | Up = noble, down = servant | Horizontal path = journey | Staircase up = ambition, down = fall | staircase vertical class, up noble down servant, 9:16 |
| 13 | Ceiling = oppression | Low ceiling = trapped | Wide walls = trapped | Low ceiling pressing down | ceiling pressing down, vertical oppression, 9:16 |
| 14 | Canopy = shelter | Overhead cover = safe | Horizontal shelter | Tree canopy above = safety | canopy overhead shelter, vertical safety, 9:16 |
| 15 | Fall = defeat | Downward = lost | Running away = defeat | Falling down frame = defeat | falling down frame, vertical defeat, 9:16 |
