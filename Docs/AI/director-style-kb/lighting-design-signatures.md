## 1. 灯光设计核心方法论

### 1.1 光线六维度控制

| 维度 | 控制 | 情绪效果 | AI提示词参数 |
|------|------|---------|------------|
| 方向 | 正面/侧面/逆光/顶光/底光 | 正面=平/侧面=深度/逆=神圣/顶=压迫/底=恐怖 | front light / side light / backlight / top light / under light |
| 质量 | 硬光/柔光/混合 | 硬=威胁/柔=亲密/混合=复杂 | hard light / soft light / mixed quality |
| 色温 | 暖(2700K)/中性(5600K)/冷(8000K+) | 暖=安全/中性=真实/冷=疏离 | warm 2700K / neutral 5600K / cold 8000K+ |
| 强度 | 高/中/低/极低 | 高=暴露/低=隐藏/极低=恐惧 | high intensity / low / minimal / candle-only |
| 运动 | 静止/闪烁/旋转/渐变 | 静=稳定/闪=不安/旋=疯狂/渐=转变 | static / flickering / rotating / gradual shift |
| 来源 | 自然/实用/舞台/魔法 | 自然=真实/实用=在场/舞台=人工/魔法=超自然 | natural / practical / theatrical / magical source |

### 1.2 情绪→光线映射(中世纪西幻专用)

| 情绪 | 光线方案 | AI提示词 |
|------|---------|---------|
| 恐惧 | 低强度+硬光+冷色温+闪烁+底光 | low intensity hard cold flickering under-light, fear lighting |
| 敬畏 | 高逆光+柔散+暖金+体积光 | high backlight soft warm golden volumetric, awe lighting |
| 孤独 | 单侧窗光+半脸+冷+长影 | single window light, half-face, cold, long shadow, solitude lighting |
| 安全 | 暖壁炉+柔散+多光源+自然 | warm fireplace soft multiple sources natural, safety lighting |
| 威胁 | 硬侧光+深影+冷+唯一光源 | hard side light, deep shadow, cold, single source, threat lighting |
| 神秘 | 低强度+侧光+雾+体积+冷蓝 | low intensity side, fog, volumetric, cold blue, mystery lighting |
| 暴力 | 硬光+高对比+红/橙+闪烁 | hard light, high contrast, red/orange, flickering, violence lighting |
| 神圣 | 过曝逆光+柔散+金+高 | overexposed backlight soft golden high, sacred lighting |
| 疯狂 | 不规则+闪烁+色温跳变+旋转 | irregular flickering, color temp jump, rotating, madness lighting |
| 悲伤 | 低平光+灰+无方向+过曝 | low flat grey, no direction, overexposed, sadness lighting |

## 3. 光线+场景直出模板

### 3.1 蜡烛唯一光源(地牢/暗黑)

`
lighting: candle-only, single source, no fill, no modern, warm 2700K, flickering
shadows: deep, long, alive, shadow moves when candle flickers
faces: half-lit, eyes gleam from dark, skin warm in candle zone, cold blue in shadow
environment: stone walls warm near candle, cold blue away, no ambient fill
prompt: candle-only single source, no fill, half-face, shadow alive, Eggers-lighting, dungeon, 9:16
`

### 3.2 黄金时段逆光(出发/旅途)

`
lighting: golden hour backlight, natural, sun low, warm 3200K, volumetric through dust
shadows: long, warm, silhouette, rim light on character
faces: backlit, silhouette, hair glow, face in shadow or slight fill
environment: warm everywhere, sky orange-gold, dust particles visible in light
prompt: golden hour backlight, rim light, silhouette, volumetric dust, Deschanel-grade, departure, 9:16
`

### 3.3 荧光体制光(审判/学院)

`
lighting: fluorescent overhead, cold 6500K, no shadow, institutional, flat
shadows: minimal, under-eye shadows, face looks tired/hollow
faces: flat, pale, no modeling, institutional cold reveals everything
environment: grey walls, white ceiling, no warmth, system light
prompt: fluorescent overhead, cold institutional, no shadow, flat, Videbaek-grade, inquisition, 9:16
`

### 3.4 火光战场(攻城/战斗)

`
lighting: fire+torches practical, warm 2500K, flickering, smoke diffuses, multiple sources
shadows: dancing, unstable, fire creates moving shadows, chaos
faces: lit by fire, warm, intense, sweat gleams, blood reflects fire
environment: smoke+fire+dark, warm near fire, dark beyond, two worlds
prompt: fire+torches practical, flickering, smoke diffusion, warm near fire dark beyond, Semler-grade, battle, 9:16
`

### 3.5 魔法光源(施法/仪式)

`
lighting: magic source, ethereal glow, cool blue-white or warm gold, pulsating, volumetric
shadows: magic light casts impossible shadows, shadow direction wrong, supernatural
faces: lit by magic, skin takes magic color, eyes reflect magic light
environment: magic light affects surroundings, particles float, air shimmers
prompt: magic light source, ethereal glow, pulsating, volumetric, impossible shadows, supernatural lighting, 9:16
`
