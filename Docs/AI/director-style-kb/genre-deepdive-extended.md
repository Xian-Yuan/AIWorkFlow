## 1. 灾难片视觉语法

### 1.1 灾难片核心美学原则

| 原则 | 含义 | 视觉手法 | 提示词 | 西幻翻译 |
|------|------|---------|--------|---------|
| 人vs自然 | 自然绝对压倒 | 人<5%帧+自然填充 | human vs nature, nature dominant, human 5% or less, overwhelming force, disaster | 龙焰/暴风/地震 |
| 秩序→混乱 | 文明的脆弱 | 前半秩序构图→后半碎片 | order to chaos, structured composition disintegrating, civilization breaking, disaster arc | 城堡的秩序→围城的混乱 |
| 微观vs宏观 | 个体故事+宏大灾难 | 交替ECU和EWS | micro-macro alternation, ECU of face then EWS of destruction, intimate within epic | 骑士的面+城堡的塌 |
| 无声预告 | 灾难前的不祥安静 | 锁定+静+小异常 | ominous calm, locked camera, still, tiny anomaly, silence before disaster | 暴风前的寂静 |
| 持续升级 | 每个镜头比上一个更糟 | 焦段渐广+运动渐快 | escalating disaster, each shot worse than last, widening lens, accelerating motion | 诅咒的扩散 |

### 1.2 灾难片导演签名

| 导演 | 代表作品 | 一句话签名 | 提示词 | 西幻翻译 |
|------|---------|-----------|--------|---------|
| Roland Emmerich | Independence Day/2012/The Day After Tomorrow | 尺度即奇观+地标毁灭+科学解释 | Emmerich scale spectacle, landmark destruction, scientific explanation, global disaster | 城堡崩塌/大陆沉没 |
| Paul Thomas Anderson | Magnolia/There Will Be Blood | 蛙雨+石油+神罚+缓慢 | PTA divine punishment, slow build to cataclysm, biblical disaster, oil as blood | 神罚/魔法灾难 |
| Werner Herzog | Grizzly Man/Fitzcarraldo/Encounters | 自然即疯狂+人vs不可理解 | Herzog nature madness, human vs incomprehensible force, ecstatic truth, abyss | 自然即诅咒/不可理解之恶 |
| Takashi Shimizu | The Grudge/Ju-On | 诅咒即灾难+蔓延+无法阻止 | Shimizu curse as disaster, spreading, unstoppable, supernatural pandemic, Japanese horror | 诅咒蔓延/无法阻止 |

### 1.3 灾难片→中世纪西幻场景模板

| 场景 | 情绪 | 导演参考 | 提示词 |
|------|------|---------|--------|
| 龙焰焚城 | 敬畏/恐惧/渺小 | Emmerich+Villeneuve | 14mm f/8 EWS, dragon fire engulfing city, human 3%, warfire red palette, Emmerich scale, Villeneuve slow reveal, overwhelming, 9:16 |
| 诅咒蔓延 | 恐惧/无力/扩散 | Shimizu+Herzog | 35mm f/4, curse spreading through village like disease, Shimizu creeping horror, Herzog incomprehensible nature, cursed green palette, 9:16 |

| 身体即叙事 | 身体运动=情绪表达 | 特写身体部位+慢动作 | ody as narrative, physical movement=emotion, CU body parts, slow-motion, athletic | 战斗即竞技/身体叙事 |

| 第一人称 | 观众=年轻人 | 50mm标准+主观镜头 | irst person, audience=young person, 50mm normal, subjective camera, coming of age | 年轻骑士视角 |

## 5. 家庭剧/客厅剧视觉语法

### 5.1 家庭剧核心美学原则

| 原则 | 含义 | 视觉手法 | 提示词 | 西幻翻译 |
|------|------|---------|--------|---------|
| 房间即关系 | 空间分隔=关系分隔 | 不同的房间=不同的关系 | 
oom as relationship, space division=emotional division, rooms have roles | 城堡的房间=家族关系 |
| 餐桌即战场 | 饭桌=权力中心 | 圆桌/长桌+坐位=阶级 | dining as battlefield, table=power center, seating=hierarchy, meal as weapon | 圆桌会议=权力场 |
| 窗内窗外 | 安全vs危险/家vs世界 | 窗=边界+窗框构图 | inside window vs outside, safety vs danger, home vs world, window frame as border | 城堡窗=安全vs危险 |
| 门的开关 | 到来vs离开=关系变化 | 门开=新关系/门关=失去 | door open=new relationship, door close=loss, threshold as change, entry and exit | 门=命运转折 |
| 遗物件 | 物件承载记忆 | 物件特写+手持+情感 | heirloom as memory, object close-up, held with emotion, inherited meaning | 家族遗物/传家剑 |
