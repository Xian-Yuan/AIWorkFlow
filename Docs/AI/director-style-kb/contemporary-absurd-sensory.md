## 1. Yorgos Lanthimos — 荒诞的精确

### 1.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 缓慢推入+锁定+广角畸变+鱼眼 | slow push-in, locked-off, wide angle distortion, fisheye, Lanthimos-style |
| 构图 | 广角畸变+鱼眼+对称+建筑压迫=社会即畸形 | wide distortion, fisheye, symmetric, architecture crush, society as deformity, Lanthimos-grade |
| 调色 | 去饱和+冷蓝+奢华金(宫廷)+白=荒诞即冷 | desaturated, cold blue, luxury gold, white, absurd as cold, Lanthimos-palette |
| 光线 | 均匀照明+自然光+蜡烛(宫廷)+低对比=日常即荒诞 | even illumination, natural, candle, low contrast, everyday as absurd, Lanthimos-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 硬切+不叠化+节奏来自荒诞对话 | hard cuts, no dissolve, rhythm from absurd dialogue, Lanthimos-editing |
| 叙事 | 规则即荒诞+语言即暴力+家庭即牢笼=社会即游戏 | rules as absurd, language as violence, family as cage, society as game, Lanthimos-narrative |

### 1.2 拉片精华

**The Favourite (2018)**: 鱼眼+广角畸变+宫廷+三女权力=权力即荒诞; 16mm胶片=粗糙的奢华
AI适配: `fisheye, wide distortion, court, three women power, power as absurd, 16mm, Lanthimos-Favourite`

**Poor Things (2023)**: 鱼眼+超现实+维多利亚+科学=解放即荒诞; 色彩从黑白→饱和=觉醒即色彩
AI适配: `fisheye, surreal, Victorian, science, liberation as absurd, B&W to saturated, Lanthimos-PoorThings`

**The Lobster (2015)**: 酒店+对称+规则(单身→动物)=社会即荒诞规则
AI适配: `hotel, symmetric, rules, single to animal, society as absurd rules, Lanthimos-Lobster`

**Dogtooth (2009)**: 围墙+隔离+创造语言=家庭即牢笼; 不出围墙=世界即谎言
AI适配: `walled, isolated, invented language, family as cage, world as lie, Lanthimos-Dogtooth`

| 西幻场景 | Lanthimos适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| 魔法规则即荒诞 | 鱼眼+广角畸变+对称+冷蓝 | fisheye, wide distortion, symmetric, cold blue, absurd rules, Lanthimos-style |
| 宫廷权力游戏 | 鱼眼+16mm+三女权力+奢华金 | fisheye, 16mm, court power, luxury gold, Lanthimos-Favourite |

### 5.1 Yorgos Lanthimos — 荒诞即正常

| 维度 | 签名特征 | 英文提示词 | 情绪内核 | 西幻适配 |
|------|---------|-----------|---------|---------|
| 构图 | 鱼眼+广角畸变+对称+荒诞规则 | fisheye, wide distortion, symmetric, absurd rules, Lanthimos-composition | 世界的规则是扭曲的 | 诅咒世界/荒诞魔法规则 |
| 光线 | 均匀+日光+无阴影=表面的正常 | even, daylight, no shadow, surface normalcy, Lanthimos-lighting | 正常外表下的恐怖 | 完美秩序下的异常 |
| 调色 | 高饱和+粉彩+不真实+冷淡 | high saturation, pastel, unreal, cold detachment, Lanthimos-grade | 色彩鲜艳但情感冷淡 | 奇异仪式/荒诞王国 |
| 运镜 | 锁定+缓慢+鱼眼漂移 | locked, slow, fisheye drift, Lanthimos-motion | 冷眼旁观荒诞 | 全片基调 |

| 叙事 | 规则即荒诞/社会即畸形/服从即反抗 | rules are absurd, society is deformed, obedience is rebellion, Lanthimos-narrative | 体制的荒诞 | 荒诞王国/诅咒规则 |
