## 1. Sergio Leone — 意大利西部/仪式暴力/极简极慢→突然爆发

### 3.2 暴力诗学公式

`
Peckinpah Violence =
  Multi-cam setup (6+ angles)
  + Slow-motion (120fps→24fps) intercut with real-time
  + Blood spray practical effect
  + Dust/debris particles
  + Sound: muted/gone, replaced by silence or music
  + Result: violence becomes beautiful tragedy, not spectacle
`

**提示词**: Peckinpah-style violence: slow-motion blood spray, multi-angle montage, dust particles, silence replacing sound, tragedy not spectacle, 120fps to 24fps

## 4. Clint Eastwood — 极简主义/沉默/美国神话解构

### 4.1 六维度签名

| 维度 | 签名特征 | 英文提示词 | 情绪内核 |
|------|---------|-----------|---------|
| 构图 | 极简/留白/单人居中;低饱和美国景 | minimal framing, negative space, lone figure centered, low saturation American landscape, Eastwood-style | 孤独/简约/压抑 |
| 光线 | 自然光/不补光/接受阴影 | vailable light only, no fill, embracing shadows, Eastwood-style naturalism | 真实/不修饰 |
| 调色 | 去饱和/棕灰/蓝灰/极少色彩 | desaturated, brown-grey, blue-grey, minimal color, Eastwood palette | 美国的灰暗面 |
| 运镜 | 极少运动/锁死/让演员动 | minimal camera movement, locked off, let actors move, Eastwood-style restraint | 克制/不干预 |
| 剪辑 | 长镜头+少切+不赶 | long takes, sparse cuts, unhurried, Eastwood rhythm, let scene breathe | 时间自然流动 |
| 叙事 | 解构神话/暴力后果/悔恨/简单道德 | deconstructing myth, violence consequences, regret, simple morality, Eastwood narrative | 神话背后是痛苦 |

### 4.2 Eastwood极简公式

`
Eastwood Frame =
  One subject + vast empty space
  + available light (no fill, no flatter)
  + desaturated palette
  + locked camera
  + silence > dialogue
  = The weight of solitude and consequence
`

**提示词**: Eastwood-style: lone figure in vast desaturated landscape, available light no fill, locked camera, silence over dialogue, weight of consequence
