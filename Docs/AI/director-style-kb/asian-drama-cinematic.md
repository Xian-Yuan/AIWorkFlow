## 1. Na Hong-jin (罗泓轸) — 恶的地理学(The Wailing / The Yellow Sea / The Chaser)

### 1.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 手持+跟拍+不稳定的凝视+突然暴力+超长镜头 | handheld, follow, unstable gaze, sudden violence, extremely long take, Na-style |
| 构图 | 山村纵深+雾+森林暗角+人脸特写=恐惧即地理 | mountain village depth, fog, forest dark, face close-up, fear as geography, Na-grade |
| 调色 | 去饱和黄绿(黄海)+深蓝红(哭声)+泥色=恶即泥土 | desaturated yellow-green, deep blue-red, mud color, evil as earth, Na-palette |
| 光线 | 雾中散射+巫师火+教堂窗光+雨=信仰即光线 | fog scatter, shaman fire, church window light, rain, faith as lighting, Na-lighting |
| 叙事 | 多重叙事+恶魔即人性+宗教=绝望+恶不可解 | multiple narratives, demon as humanity, religion as despair, evil as unsolvable, Na-narrative |

### 1.2 核心作品拉片

**The Wailing (2016)**: 巫师+日本鬼+基督教=三重信仰=恶不可解; 雨季=腐化; 小女孩=纯真即恐怖
AI适配: `shaman+Japanese demon+Christianity, three faiths, evil unsolvable, rain as corruption, girl as innocent horror, Na-Wailing`

**The Chaser (2008)**: 追=被追=空间即猎场+首尔夜+雨+连续暴力
AI适配: `chase=hunted, space as hunting ground, Seoul night, rain, continuous violence, Na-Chaser`

**The Yellow Sea (2010)**: 延边=绝望+首尔=暴力+斧头=生存+雪=冷
AI适配: `Yanbian as despair, Seoul as violence, axe as survival, snow as cold, Na-YellowSea`

### 1.3 AI短剧适配(中世纪西幻)

| 西幻场景 | Na适配 | 提示词公式 |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| 诅咒/巫术(不可解) | 雾+山村+三重信仰+恶不可解 | `fog, mountain village, multiple faiths, evil unsolvable, Na-Wailing, cursed village` |
| 追杀/猎场 | 手持跟拍+雨+夜+空间即猎场 | `handheld follow, rain, night, space as hunting ground, Na-Chaser, manhunt` |
