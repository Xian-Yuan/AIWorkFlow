## 1. Hou Hsiao-Hsien (侯孝贤) — 远观的悲悯

### 1.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 锁定+极慢推入+不手持+固定机位 | locked-off, extremely slow push-in, no handheld, fixed camera, Hou-style |
| 构图 | 远景+人在环境中极小+门框窗框+层次纵深 | distant shot, human tiny in environment, door/window frame, layered depth, Hou-grade |
| 调色 | 自然饱和+褪色+旧时光感+台湾绿 | natural saturation, faded, old time feel, Taiwan green, Hou-palette |
| 光线 | 自然光+窗光+油灯+烟雾 | natural light, window, oil lamp, smoke, Hou-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 极少剪辑+长镜头+留白+静止 | minimal cuts, long takes, emptiness, stillness, Hou-editing |
| 叙事 | 历史即个人+记忆即电影+时间即消逝 | history as personal, memory as cinema, time as fading, Hou-narrative |

### 1.2 拉片精华

**A City of Sadness (1989)**: 远景+家庭+历史=个人即历史; 窗框+门框=角色被框在历史中
AI适配: `distant shot, family, history, individual as history, window frame, Hou-CitySadness`

**The Assassin (2015)**: 远景+黑白+丝绸+烛光=武术即沉默; 极少对白=动作即语言
AI适配: `distant, B&W, silk, candlelight, martial arts as silence, minimal dialogue, Hou-Assassin`

**Flowers of Shanghai (1998)**: 油灯+丝绸+烟雾+缓慢=欲望即烟雾; 全片室内=欲望即封闭
AI适配: `oil lamp, silk, smoke, slow, desire as smoke, all interior, Hou-FlowersShanghai`

### 1.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Hou适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|--------|--------|
| 魔法暗杀(远观) | 远景+锁定+窗框+沉默 | distant shot, locked-off, window frame, silence, Hou-Assassin |

## 6. 具体影片拉片

### 6.1 Hou Hsiao-hsien — 长镜头中的历史/窗=时代/静即叙事

| 帧 | 景别 | 内容 | 技法 | 提示词提取 |
|------|------|------|------|-----------|
| 窗内构图 | MS | 人物在窗框内/室外看不到 | 窗=时代限制/看不到外面 | figure in window frame, outside unseen, window=era limit, Hou |
| 长镜头 | WS | 固定/人物进出/时间流 | 不切/让时间自己流 | fixed wide, characters enter/exit, time flows, no cuts, let time pass, Hou |
| 火车窗 | MS | 移动中的窗/风景变/人不变 | 窗+运动=时代在变 | train window, landscape changes, person stays, window+movement=era changes, Hou |
| 沉默间隔 | CU | 面部/不说话/让沉默说话 | 锁定/沉默=历史太重 | face CU, not speaking, let silence speak, locked, silence=history too heavy, Hou |

**Hou→西幻**: 修道院窗内/长镜头=时间流/沉默=诅咒太重无法说

### 6.2 Kore-eda — 日常即深刻/食物即爱/不戏剧化

| 帧 | 景别 | 内容 | 技法 | 提示词提取 |
|------|------|------|------|-----------|
| 家庭餐桌 | MS | 普通饭/筷子/对话/日常 | 手持观察/日常即深刻 | family table, ordinary meal, chopsticks, handheld observe, daily=profound, Kore-eda |
| 孩子 | CU | 孩子/天真/不理解大人世界 | 锁定/天真=真相 | child CU, innocent, doesn't understand adult world, locked, innocence=truth, Kore-eda |
| 食物特写 | ECU | 煮饭/蒸汽/食材=爱 | 极近/食物=关怀=爱 | food ECU, cooking, steam, ingredients=love, extreme close, food=care, Kore-eda |
| 沉默散步 | WS | 两人走/不说/关系在沉默中 | 手持跟/不赶/关系=在场 | two walking, no words, relationship in silence, handheld follow, no rush, Kore-eda |

**Kore-eda→西幻**: 骑士营地的日常/食物=关怀/沉默=最深的情谊

### 6.3 PTA — 桑卡式的漫游/长镜头漫游/不解释

| 帧 | 景别 | 内容 | 技法 | 提示词提取 |
|------|------|------|------|-----------|
| 漫游序列 | WS→MS | 角色穿过空间/不切/发现 | 长镜漫游/不赶/生活流 | character wanders through space, no cuts, discovery, long-take wander, PTA |
| 群戏 | WS | 多人/混乱/能量/不控制 | 手持+多机/让混乱发生 | ensemble, chaos, energy, handheld+multi-cam, let chaos happen, PTA |
| 突然亲密 | CU | 两人在混乱中突然对视 | 从混乱到亲密的突然 | sudden intimacy in chaos, two lock eyes, chaos→intimacy, PTA |
| 不解释结尾 | WS | 画面/音乐/不回答 | 锁定/不解释=尊重观众 | unresolved ending, image+music, no answer, locked, PTA |

**PTA→西幻**: 市集漫游/混乱中的突然亲密/不解释的魔法=尊重观众

### 6.4 Coen Brothers — 命运即讽刺/类型即玩笑/每帧是设计

| 帧 | 景别 | 内容 | 技法 | 提示词提取 |
|------|------|------|------|-----------|
| 命运反讽 | WS | 角色做了"正确"选择→更惨 | 锁定/命运=讽刺 | character makes right choice→worse, fate=irony, locked, Coen |
| 类型玩笑 | MS | 看似西部/实际黑色/看似黑色/实际喜剧 | 类型混搭/每帧都在设计 | genre玩笑, looks western/is noir, looks noir/is comedy, Coen |
| 精确构图 | MS | 每帧精心设计/不留随机 | 精确/每帧=设计=控制 | every frame designed, no randomness, precision, Coen |
| 突然暴力 | CU | 平静对话→突然枪/沉默 | 平静→暴力=Coen签名 | calm dialogue→sudden gun, silence, calm→violence=Coen signature |

**Coen→西幻**: 骑士做正确选择→诅咒更重/看似英雄实际讽刺/突然暴力=命运无常

## 7. 亚洲独立导演关键场景模板(直出提示词)

### 7.1 Hou式长镜头(修道院日常)

`
WS: monastery refectory, window frame, candle, fixed wide, time flows, Hou, 9:16
MS: monk at window, outside unseen, window=era limit, Hou
CU: face, silence, history too heavy to speak, locked, let silence be, Hou
WS: fixed, characters enter/exit, no cuts, time passes naturally, Hou
grade: natural desat, warm candle zone, cold beyond window, Hou palette
style: Hou long-take, window=limit, silence=weight, time flows without cut
`

`
WS: campfire, knights eating, ordinary, handheld observe, Kore-eda, 9:16
MS: two knights sharing bread, food=care, daily=profound, Kore-eda
ECU: campfire stew, steam, ingredients=brotherhood, Kore-eda
CU: face, quiet, relationship in silence, locked, Kore-eda
grade: warm campfire, natural, Kodak 500T, Kore-eda palette
style: Kore-eda daily=profound, food=love, silence=deep bond, no drama
`

### 7.3 Coen式命运讽刺(诅咒反讽)

`
WS: castle corridor, knight makes right choice, fate=irony, Coen, 9:16

[SUDDEN] MS: violence erupts, calm→chaos, Coen

style: Coen irony, right choice=worse fate, sudden violence, genre 玩笑
