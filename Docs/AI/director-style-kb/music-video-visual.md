## 1. David Fincher MV时代 — 控制的极致(Express Yourself / Vogue / Criminal / Bad Blood)

### 1.1 签名美学(MV专用)

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 精确轨道+锁定+缓慢推入+不浪费一帧 | precise track, locked, slow push-in, no wasted frame, Fincher-MV-style |
| 构图 | 居中+对称+几何+身体即建筑+群舞即图案 | centered, symmetric, geometric, body as architecture, dance as pattern, Fincher-MV-grade |
| 调色 | 去饱和+冷+银+金+黑=控制即颜色 | desaturated, cold, silver, gold, black, control as color, Fincher-MV-palette |
| 光线 | 精确光源+轮廓光+无溢出+硬边=控制即光线 | precise source, rim light, no spill, hard edge, control as light, Fincher-MV-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 精确到帧+音画同步+无多余+节奏即数学 | frame-precise, sync sound, no excess, rhythm as math, Fincher-MV-editing |

### 1.2 核心MV拉片

**Madonna "Express Yourself" (1989)**: 工业+身体+齿轮=权力即机器; 去饱和+硬光+几何
AI适配: `industrial, body, gears, power as machine, desaturated hard light geometric, Fincher-Express`

**Madonna "Vogue" (1990)**: 黑白+黄金时代+舞蹈即雕塑+框架构图=致敬即风格
AI适配: `B&W, golden age, dance as sculpture, frame within frame, homage as style, Fincher-Vogue`

**Nine Inch Nails "Criminal" (2005)**: 机器+身体+旋转+去饱和=暴力即机器
AI适配: `machine, body, rotation, desaturated, violence as machine, Fincher-Criminal`

**Taylor Swift "Bad Blood" (2015)**: 赛博+群女+慢动作+霓虹=女即战士
AI适配: `cyber, female ensemble, slow motion, neon, woman as warrior, Fincher-BadBlood`

### 1.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Fincher-MV适配 | 提示词公式 |
|----------|---------------|-----------|
| 魔法战斗(精确) | 精确轨道+慢动作+去饱和+几何 | `precise track, slow motion, desaturated, geometric, Fincher-MV-grade, magic combat` |
| 仪式/群舞 | 对称+身体即建筑+群舞即图案 | `symmetric, body as architecture, dance as pattern, Fincher-MV-ritual` |
| 黑白致敬 | 黑白+框架构图+雕塑+黄金时代 | `B&W, frame within frame, sculpture, golden age, Fincher-Vogue, homage` |
