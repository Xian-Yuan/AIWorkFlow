## 1. Ingmar Bergman — 灵魂的审讯官

### 1.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 极慢推入面部+锁定+不手持+极少运动 | extremely slow push-in face, locked-off, no handheld, minimal movement, Bergman-style |
| 构图 | 面部特写占满画面+双人对视+半脸光=灵魂即面孔 | face fills frame, dual gaze, half-face light, soul as face, Bergman-grade |
| 调色 | 黑白高对比+灰阶丰富+极少彩色=灵魂即灰度 | B&W high contrast, rich grey scale, minimal color, soul as greyscale, Bergman-palette |
| 光线 | 窗光+半脸明暗+深阴影=上帝即沉默 | window light, half-face chiaroscuro, deep shadows, God as silence, Bergman-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 极少剪辑+长镜头+节奏来自沉默 | minimal cuts, long takes, rhythm from silence, Bergman-editing |
| 叙事 | 信仰危机+死亡焦虑+人际即地狱+梦即真相 | faith crisis, death anxiety, others as hell, dream as truth, Bergman-narrative |

### 1.2 拉片精华

**The Seventh Seal (1957)**: 海滩骑士vs死神下棋=死亡即日常; 地平线上死神剪影=死亡即地平线
AI适配: `beach, knight vs Death chess, silhouette on horizon, death as everyday, Bergman-Seal`

**Persona (1966)**: 双人脸逐渐融合=身份即镜像; 胶片烧毁+画面撕裂=身份即崩溃
AI适配: `dual face merge, identity as mirror, film burns, frame tears, identity collapse, Bergman-Persona`

**Fanny and Alexander (1982)**: 主教家冷蓝对称=宗教即牢笼; 家暖金混乱=家庭即自由
AI适配: `bishop house cold blue symmetric vs family home warm gold chaotic, Bergman-Fanny`

### 1.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Bergman适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|------------|--------|
| 骑士质疑信仰 | 极慢推入面部+半脸光+窗光 | extremely slow push-in face, half-face light, window, faith crisis, Bergman-style |
| 死亡即日常 | 剪影+地平线+下棋 | silhouette, horizon, chess with death, death as everyday, Bergman-Seal |
| 身份融合/附身 | 双人脸融合+胶片撕裂 | dual face merge, film tears, identity collapse, possession, Bergman-Persona |

## 2. Lars von Trier — 折磨的美学

### 2.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 手持(Dogme95)+跳切+急推+数码粗糙 | handheld Dogme95, jump cut, crash zoom, digital rough, Trier-style |
| 构图 | 舞台式(黑暗中只照亮舞台)+框架+章节标题 | stage framing, only stage lit, framed, chapter titles, Trier-grade |
| 调色 | 去饱和+冷蓝+唯一暖色=痛苦即冷 | desaturated, cold blue, only warm is pain, Trier-palette |
| 光线 | 舞台聚光+自然光(Dogme)+过曝=折磨即照明 | stage spotlight, natural Dogme, overexposed, torture as lighting, Trier-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 跳切+章节分割+时间压缩=碎片即创伤 | jump cut, chapter divide, time compression, fragments as trauma, Trier-editing |
| 叙事 | 女性受难+社会即折磨+牺牲即救赎 | female suffering, society as torture, sacrifice as redemption, Trier-narrative |

### 2.2 拉片精华

**Dogville (2003)**: 无布景只有地面画线=社会即虚构; 没有墙壁=隐私即幻觉
AI适配: `no set, only floor lines, society as fiction, no walls, privacy as illusion, Trier-Dogville`

**Melancholia (2011)**: 慢动作+过曝+世界毁灭=抑郁即末日; Justine在绝望中平静面对末日=抑郁即超能力
AI适配: `slow motion, overexposed, world ends, depression as apocalypse, calm in doom, Trier-Melancholia`

**Dancer in the Dark (2000)**: Dogme手持+跳切现实 vs 百老汇歌舞幻想=两个世界
AI适配: `Dogme handheld reality vs Broadway musical fantasy, two worlds, Trier-Dancer`

### 2.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Trier适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|----------|--------|
| 魔法代价的折磨 | 舞台聚光+冷蓝+章节分割 | stage spotlight, cold blue, chapter divide, magic cost torture, Trier-style |
| 世界末日/灾变 | 慢动作+过曝+平静面对 | slow motion, overexposed, calm in doom, apocalypse, Trier-Melancholia |
| 社会即牢笼 | 无布景/极简+地面线+无墙 | minimal set, floor lines only, no walls, society as cage, Trier-Dogville |

## 3. Agnes Varda — 游荡的凝视

### 3.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 手持漂移+随意跟拍+跳跃式+发现式 | handheld drift, casual follow, jump, discovery mode, Varda-style |
| 构图 | 街头抓拍+非对称+日常即电影+拼贴 | street capture, asymmetric, everyday as cinema, collage, Varda-grade |
| 调色 | 自然饱和+生活色+偶尔黑白=记忆即褪色 | natural saturation, life colors, occasional B&W, memory as fading, Varda-palette |
| 剪辑 | 跳切+拼贴+照片插入+自由组合 | jump cut, collage, photo insert, free assembly, Varda-editing |
| 叙事 | 游荡即叙事+边缘人即主角+时间即海滩 | wandering as narrative, marginal as protagonist, time as beachcombing, Varda-narrative |

### 3.2 拉片精华

**Cleo from 5 to 7 (1962)**: 实时90分钟=等待即电影; 镜子+帽子+歌曲=女性即被观看
AI适配: `real-time, mirror, hat, song, woman as observed, Varda-Cleo`

**The Gleaners and I (2000)**: 捡拾+边缘人+废弃=消费即浪费; 数字手持+自我拍摄=纪录片即自画像
AI适配: `gleaners, marginal, discarded, digital handheld self-filming, Varda-Gleaners`

### 3.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Varda适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|---------|--------|
| 流民/边缘人 | 手持漂移+街头+发现式 | handheld drift, street, discovery mode, marginal people, Varda-style |
| 等待/焦虑 | 实时+镜子+日常即电影 | real-time, mirror, everyday as cinema, waiting anxiety, Varda-Cleo |
| 记忆拼贴 | 黑白闪回+照片插入+拼贴 | B&W flashback, photo insert, collage, memory fragments, Varda-collage |

## 4. Wim Wenders — 旅途的朝圣者

### 4.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 长镜头+缓慢横移+公路跟拍+航拍 | long take, slow lateral, road follow, aerial, Wenders-style |
| 构图 | 公路水平线+天空占半+人在边缘+负空间 | road horizon, sky half, human at edge, negative space, Wenders-grade |
| 调色 | 自然饱和+公路蓝+沙漠橙+城市霓虹 | natural saturation, road blue, desert orange, city neon, Wenders-palette |
| 光线 | 自然光+黄昏+霓虹+车灯=旅途即光线 | natural light, dusk, neon, headlights, journey as light, Wenders-lighting |
| 叙事 | 旅途即叙事+陌生人即天使+照片即记忆 | journey as narrative, strangers as angels, photos as memory, Wenders-narrative |

### 4.2 拉片精华

**Wings of Desire (1987)**: 天使视角黑白高调俯瞰 vs 人类视角彩色暖体验; 天使变人时黑白转彩色=选择即感受
AI适配: `angel POV B&W high-key overhead, human POV color warm, B&W to color = choosing to feel, Wenders-Wings`

**Paris, Texas (1984)**: 沙漠+公路+霓虹+沉默=美国即荒芜; 逆光+过曝+沙漠=失去即风景
AI适配: `desert, road, neon, silence, backlight overexposed, loss as landscape, Wenders-ParisTexas`

### 4.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Wenders适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|------------|--------|
| 天使/精灵观察人类 | 黑白高调俯瞰转彩色暖体验 | angel POV B&W overhead, human POV color warm, Wenders-Wings |
| 旅途/朝圣 | 公路+天空半+黄昏+沉默 | road, sky half, dusk, silence, pilgrimage, Wenders-style |
| 失去即风景 | 逆光+过曝+沙漠/荒原 | backlight, overexposed, wasteland, loss as landscape, Wenders-ParisTexas |

## 5. 欧洲大师完整6维度签名速查

> 补充上面4位导演的完整6维度(构图/光线/调色/运镜/剪辑/叙事)统一格式

### 5.1 Ingmar Bergman — 灵魂的审讯官(完整)

| 维度 | 签名特征 | 英文提示词 | 情绪内核 | 西幻适配 |
|------|---------|-----------|---------|---------|
| 构图 | 极慢推入面部+半脸光+特写=灵魂审讯 | extreme slow push to face, half-face light, close-up, soul interrogation, Bergman-composition | 灵魂被审视 | 告解/祈祷/审判 |
| 光线 | 单侧窗光+半脸明暗+北欧冬光 | single window light, half-face chiaroscuro, Nordic winter light, Bergman-lighting(Nykvist) | 灵魂分裂/信仰危机 | 教堂/密室/独处 |
| 调色 | 黑白为主+后期极少色彩+突然红=情感爆发 | black and white, rare color, sudden red = emotional eruption, Bergman-grade | 存在困境/极简 | 信仰/死亡/沉默 |
| 运镜 | 极慢推入+锁定凝视+不移动 | extreme slow push-in, locked stare, no movement, Bergman-motion | 时间凝固 | 告解/临终/等待 |
| 剪辑 | 极少切+长镜+沉默>对话 | minimal cuts, long takes, silence > dialogue, Bergman-editing | 沉默即深意 | 全片基调 |
| 叙事 | 信仰即怀疑/沉默即对话/死亡即上帝 | faith is doubt, silence is dialogue, death is God, Bergman-narrative | 存在主义 | 信仰危机/死亡面前 |

**Bergman美学速用公式**:
```
"Bergman-grade, extreme slow push to face half-face chiaroscuro, 
single window light Nordic winter, black and white rare color sudden red, 
locked stare silence, faith is doubt, soul interrogation"
```

**关键场景: The Seventh Seal — 骑士与死神下棋**

| 镜号 | 景别 | 运镜 | 光线 | 调色 | 提示词 |
|------|------|------|------|------|--------|
| 1 | WS 海滩全景 | 锁定 | 过曝光+漫射 | 黑白高对比 | `WS beach, locked, overexposed diffused, B&W high contrast, Seventh-Seal` |
| 2 | MS 骑士+死神对坐 | 缓慢推入 | 侧光+半脸 | 黑白+两人半脸明暗 | `MS knight and Death, slow push-in, side light half-face, B&W dual chiaroscuro` |
| 3 | CU 骑士面部 | 极慢推入 | 窗光+半脸 | 黑白+唯一亮侧 | `CU knight face, extreme slow push, window light half-face, B&W sole bright side` |
| 4 | ECU 棋盘+手 | 锁定 | 侧光 | 黑白+手阴影 | `ECU chessboard hands, locked, side light, B&W hand shadow, fate in play` |
| 5 | WS 死神轮廓+海 | 缓慢拉出 | 逆光剪影 | 黑白+死神黑影 | `WS Death silhouette sea, slow pull-out, backlight, B&W Death black shadow` |

**西幻翻译**: 骑士与死神下棋→骑士与命运对峙
`knight and Death chess → knight versus fate, destiny game, Bergman-fantasy-chess`
