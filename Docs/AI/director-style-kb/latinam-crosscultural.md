## 1. Alejandro Gonzalez Inarritu — 连接的悲剧

### 1.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 长镜头+手持+环绕+一镜到底(鸟人) | long take, handheld, orbit, one-take illusion, Inarritu-style |
| 构图 | 紧跟面部+走廊纵深+人群挤压 | tight on face, corridor depth, crowd crush, Inarritu-grade |
| 调色 | 去饱和+冷蓝+沙漠橙+血红 | desaturated, cold blue, desert orange, blood red, Inarritu-palette |
| 光线 | 自然光+舞台光(鸟人)+沙漠硬光 | natural light, stage light, desert hard light, Inarritu-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 交叉剪辑(巴别塔)+伪一镜到底(鸟人)+时间跳跃 | cross-cutting Babel, fake one-take Birdman, time jump, Inarritu-editing |
| 叙事 | 多线交汇(蝴蝶效应)+连接即悲剧+自我即毁灭 | multi-thread converge, connection as tragedy, self as destruction, Inarritu-narrative |

### 1.2 拉片精华

**Babel (2006)**: 四地(摩洛哥/日本/墨西哥/美国)交叉=一枪连接全球; 子弹即连接
AI适配: `four locations cross-cut, one bullet connects world, connection as tragedy, Inarritu-Babel`

**Birdman (2014)**: 伪一镜到底+走廊+后台+舞台=演员即角色; 长镜头穿过走廊=现实即表演
AI适配: `fake one-take, corridor, backstage, stage, actor as character, reality as performance, Inarritu-Birdman`

**The Revenant (2015)**: 自然光+长镜头+熊攻击+雪+呼吸=生存即呼吸
AI适配: `natural light only, long take, bear attack, snow, breathing, survival as breath, Inarritu-Revenant`

### 1.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Inarritu适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| 多线交汇的阴谋 | 交叉剪辑+蝴蝶效应+连接即悲剧 | cross-cutting, butterfly effect, connection as tragedy, Inarritu-Babel |
| 魔法的代价(一镜到底感) | 伪一镜到底+走廊+后台 | fake one-take, corridor, backstage, Inarritu-Birdman |
| 荒野求生 | 自然光+长镜头+雪+呼吸 | natural light only, long take, snow, breathing, survival, Inarritu-Revenant |

## 2. Alfonso Cuaron — 连续的沉浸

### 2.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 一镜到底+长镜头+手持+环绕+不切 | one-take, long take, handheld, orbit, no cuts, Cuaron-style |
| 构图 | 深景深+前景运动+环境叙事+人在世界中 | deep focus, foreground motion, environment narrative, human in world, Cuaron-grade |
| 调色 | 冷蓝+暖金+去饱和+水色 | cold blue, warm gold, desaturated, water tones, Cuaron-palette |
| 光线 | 自然光+窗光+长镜头内光线变化 | natural light, window, light changes within long take, Cuaron-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 极少剪辑+长镜头代替蒙太奇+时间连续 | minimal cuts, long take replaces montage, time continuous, Cuaron-editing |
| 叙事 | 成长即失去+重力即命运+孩子视角 | growth as loss, gravity as fate, child POV, Cuaron-narrative |

### 2.2 拉片精华

**Children of Men (2006)**: 一镜到底战区+手持+深景深=观众即在场; 未来即现在(无科幻感)
AI适配: `one-take war zone, handheld, deep focus, viewer present, future as present, Cuaron-ChildrenMen`

**Gravity (2013)**: 一镜到底13分钟+太空+旋转=失重即恐惧; 长镜头=空间即时间
AI适配: `one-take 13min, space, rotation, weightlessness as fear, Cuaron-Gravity`

**Roma (2018)**: 黑白+自然光+长镜头+女佣视角=阶级即日常
AI适配: `B&W, natural light, long take, maid POV, class as everyday, Cuaron-Roma`

### 2.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Cuaron适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|----------|--------|
| 战场/围城沉浸 | 一镜到底+手持+深景深 | one-take, handheld, deep focus, battlefield immersion, Cuaron-style |
| 魔法失重/飞行 | 长镜头+旋转+空间即时间 | long take, rotation, space as time, weightless magic, Cuaron-Gravity |

## 4. Park Chan-wook — 复仇的精密(Oldboy/Lady Vengeance)

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 精确轨道+环绕+急推+慢动作 | precision track, orbit, crash zoom, slow motion, Park-style |
| 构图 | 对称+框架+色彩编码+极端构图 | symmetric, framed, color-coded, extreme composition, Park-grade |
| 调色 | 冷蓝+血红+绿+去饱和=复仇即颜色 | cold blue, blood red, green, desaturated, revenge as color, Park-palette |

| 剪辑 | 精确切+匹配剪辑+时间压缩 | precise cut, match cut, time compression, Park-editing |
| 叙事 | 复仇即循环+代价即自我+暴力即仪式 | revenge as cycle, cost as self, violence as ritual, Park-narrative |

### 4.2 拉片精华

**Oldboy (2003)**: 走廊长镜头锤子战=暴力即通道; 匹配剪辑(蚂蚁→城市)=微观即宏观

**Stoker (2013)**: 希区柯克式+慢动作+鞋子+钢琴=成长即暴力
