### 2.2 Chazelle节奏运镜公式

`
Chazelle Rhythm Sequence =
  Slow establishing (30s, locked)
  + Tempo builds (camera starts moving, 15s cuts)
  + Acceleration (orbital, push-in, 5s cuts)
  + Climax (ECU + slow-mo + music peak)
  + Hard stop (silence, locked, face)
  + Aftermath (one long take, breathing)
`

**提示词**: Chazelle rhythm: slow establish, tempo build, accelerating orbit, ECU climax slow-mo, hard stop silence, aftermath breathing, 9:16 vertical
