## 1. Akira Kurosawa — 雨与风的导演

### 1.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 多机位同时拍摄+长焦压缩+横向轨道+天气运动 | multi-camera, telephoto compression, lateral track, weather movement, Kurosawa-style |
| 构图 | 深景深三区构图(前景+中景+远景同时叙事)+群体动态 | deep focus three-zone, foreground/midground/background narrative, group dynamics, Kurosawa-grade |
| 调色 | 后期彩色: 自然饱和+红+蓝+对比 | 
atural saturation, red/blue contrast, Kurosawa-palette |
| 光线 | 自然光+逆光+雨中背光剪影=英雄即风雨 | 
atural light, backlight, rain silhouette, hero is storm, Kurosawa-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 切在动作上(不切静止)=节奏来自运动 | cut on action not stillness, rhythm from movement, Kurosawa-editing |
| 叙事 | 武士道+集体vs个人+雨=净化+风=命运 | ushido, collective vs individual, rain as purification, wind as fate |

### 1.2 拉片精华

**Seven Samurai (1954)**:
- 暴雨决斗: 雨中背光剪影+泥+慢动作=战斗即仪式
- 多机位: 同时拍3台摄影机，剪辑选择=不是后期才想怎么切
- 深景深: 前景村民+中景武士+远景山=三个故事同时讲
- AI适配: ain silhouette, mud, slow motion, multi-camera, deep focus three-zone, battle as ritual

**Ran (1985)**:
- 色彩编码: 三个儿子三色旗(黄/红/蓝)=身份即颜色
- 战场: 无声+只有风+旗帜飘=战争即风景
- AI适配: color-coded armies, silent battle, only wind, flags fluttering, war as landscape, Kurosawa-Ran

**Rashomon (1950)**:
- 多重视角: 同一事件4种叙述=真相不可知
- 光线: 直射阳光穿林=真相刺目但被遮蔽
- AI适配: sunlight through forest, four perspectives, truth unknowable, Rashomon-effect

### 1.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Kurosawa适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| 骑士决斗 | 雨中背光剪影+泥+慢动作 | ain silhouette, mud, slow motion, duel as ritual, Kurosawa-style |
| 军队色彩编码 | 不同阵营不同颜色旗帜 | color-coded armies, flags as identity, battle as landscape |
| 真相不可知 | 多视角+阳光穿林 | sunlight through canopy, multiple perspectives, truth unknowable |

## 2. Steven Spielberg — 情感的工程师

### 2.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 斯坦尼康跟拍+缓慢环绕+人物推入+大升降 | Steadicam follow, slow orbit, character push-in, grand crane, Spielberg-style |
| 构图 | 画面内光源(灯笼/火/月亮)+人脸+光影=情感即光 | in-frame light source, face, light and shadow, emotion is light, Spielberg-grade |
| 调色 | 暖金为主+阴影深蓝=美国家庭色板 | warm gold dominant, deep blue shadows, American family palette |
| 光线 | 镜头光晕+逆光+魔法时刻=奇迹即光 | lens flare, backlight, magic hour, wonder is light, Spielberg-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 反应镜头(人脸)=观众的情感锚点 | eaction shots, face as emotional anchor, Spielberg-editing |
| 叙事 | 童心+家庭+奇迹+失去+找回 | childhood, family, wonder, loss, recovery |

### 2.2 拉片精华

**Saving Private Ryan (1998)** — DP: Janusz Kaminski
- 开场: 手持+脱轴+过曝+去机盖=战争的混乱
- 关键技法: 去掉摄影机防光罩=光晕+过曝=D-Day的刺目
- AI适配: handheld,脱轴, overexposed, no matte box, D-Day chaos, Kaminski-style

**Schindler's List (1993)** — DP: Janusz Kaminski
- 黑白中唯一红衣女孩=唯一有颜色=唯一有名字的死者
- AI适配: lack and white, single red coat girl, color as identity, Schindler red

**Jurassic Park (1993)**:
- 第一次见恐龙: 缓慢推入+反应镜头+John Williams音乐=敬畏的完整工程
- AI适配: slow reveal, reaction shot, awe engineering, first sighting of wonder, Spielberg-awe

### 2.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Spielberg适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| 第一次见巨龙 | 缓慢推入+反应镜头+逆光 | slow reveal, reaction shot, backlight, first sighting of dragon, Spielberg-awe |
| 战场混乱 | 手持+过曝+脱轴 | handheld, overexposed,脱轴, battlefield chaos, Kaminski-style |
| 唯一希望的象征 | 画面中唯一暖色/唯一亮光 | single warm element in cold frame, color as hope, Spielberg-red |

## 3. Wong Kar-wai — 时间的碎片

### 3.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 手持漂移+慢速(步印)+偏轴跟拍+无轨道 | handheld drift, step-printed slow, off-axis follow, no track, Wong-Kar-wai-style |
| 构图 | 偏轴+前景遮挡+裁切人脸+镜子反射=欲念即不可见 | off-axis, foreground obstruction, cropped face, mirror reflection, desire is invisible |
| 调色 | 霓虹暖冷+绿红对比+过饱和+褪色交替 | 
eon warm-cool, green-red contrast, oversaturated vs faded, Wong-palette |
| 光线 | 霓虹+灯笼+镜面反射+烟雾=欲望的光 | 
eon, lantern, mirror reflection, smoke, light of desire, Doyle-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 步印(跳帧)+慢动作+叠化=时间即碎片 | step-printing, slow motion, dissolve, time as fragments, Wong-editing |
| 叙事 | 错失+时间差+回忆即现实+爱情即遗憾 | missed connections, time gap, memory as reality, love as regret |

### 3.2 拉片精华

**In the Mood for Love (2000)** — DP: Christopher Doyle / Mark Lee
- 走廊: 偏轴+慢速+旗袍红+墙壁绿=欲念即墙壁
- 时间: 钟表特写+慢动作+步印=时间流逝即爱情
- AI适配: off-axis corridor, step-printed slow, red dress green wall, time as love, Wong-Doyle

**2046 (2004)**:
- 霓虹+火车+雪+叠化=未来即过去的回声
- AI适配: 
eon, train, snow, dissolve, future as echo of past, 2046

**Chungking Express (1994)**:
- 手持+快速+便利店霓虹+步印=都市即速度
- AI适配: handheld, fast, convenience store neon, step-printed, urban as speed, Chungking

### 3.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Wong Kar-wai适配 | 提示词 |

| 禁忌之恋 | 偏轴+前景遮挡+暖冷对比 | off-axis, foreground obstruction, warm-cool contrast, forbidden love, Wong-Doyle |

ature as narrative, humans small in nature, God's eye view |

**The Thin Red Line (1998)**:
- 战争=自然+士兵在草丛+风吹=战争即风景

**Days of Heaven (1978)** — DP: Néstor Almendros
- 魔法时刻: 全片只在日出日落拍摄=光即时间即叙事

## 5. Christopher Nolan — 时间的建筑师

### 5.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | IMAX大广角+精确轨道+升降+环绕+无手持 | IMAX wide, precision track, crane, orbit, no handheld, Nolan-style |
| 构图 | 对称+破对称+IMAX极宽+巨幅画幅+深景深 | symmetry then broken, IMAX ultra-wide, monumental, deep focus, Nolan-grade |
| 调色 | 冷蓝+暖橙对分+去饱和+高对比 | cold blue vs warm orange split, desaturated, high contrast, Nolan-palette |
| 光线 | 冷暖对分+自然与人工并存+IMAX深景深 | cold-warm split, natural+artificial coexist, IMAX deep focus, Hoytema-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 交叉剪辑+时间线重叠+倒叙=时间即结构 | cross-cutting, overlapping timelines, reverse chronology, time as structure, Nolan-editing |
| 叙事 | 时间即主角+规则即叙事+烧脑=观众参与 | 	ime as protagonist, rules as narrative, puzzle = audience participation |

### 5.2 拉片精华

**Dunkirk (2017)** — DP: Hoyte van Hoytema
- 三时间线: 1周陆地+1天海上+1小时空中=同时剪辑=时间压缩
- IMAX: 全片70mm+IMAX=极致画幅+深景深+颗粒感
- AI适配: 	hree timelines cross-cut, IMAX 70mm, deep focus, time compression, Nolan-Dunkirk

**Oppenheimer (2023)** — DP: Hoyte van Hoytema
- 黑白vs彩色: 彩色=主观/黑白=客观=视角即颜色
- 闪烁: 核爆前的静默=恐惧来自等待
- AI适配: color=subjective, B&W=objective, flash before blast, silence as fear, Nolan-Oppenheimer

**The Dark Knight (2008)**:
- 开场: 银行抢劫=交叉剪辑+IMAX+精确轨道=混乱中的秩序
- AI适配: cross-cutting bank heist, IMAX, precision track, order within chaos, Nolan-DarkKnight
