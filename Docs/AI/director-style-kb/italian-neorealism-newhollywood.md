## 2. Francis Ford Coppola — 家族即悲剧/黑暗即权力/歌剧即暴力

|------|---------|-----------|---------|

| 光线 | 极暗+窗光/烛光/橙灯/黑暗吞噬边缘 | extreme dark + window light, candlelight, orange lamp, darkness swallows edges, Coppola-style | 权力在黑暗中 |
