## 1. Safdie Brothers — 不可呼吸的紧迫(Uncut Gems/Good Time)

### 1.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 手持+紧贴面部+快速跟拍+不切+蛇眼 | handheld, tight on face, fast follow, no cuts, SnorrCam, Safdie-style |
| 构图 | 面部占满+走廊挤压+人群包围=焦虑即空间 | face fills frame, corridor crush, crowd surround, anxiety as space, Safdie-grade |
| 调色 | 去饱和+霓虹暖+冷白+宝石色=贪婪即颜色 | desaturated, neon warm, cold white, gem tones, greed as color, Safdie-palette |
| 光线 | 霓虹+珠宝灯光+荧光+走廊灯=紧迫即灯光 | neon, jewelry light, fluorescent, corridor light, urgency as light, Safdie-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 快切+不呼吸+音乐叠加+声画不同步=焦虑即节奏 | fast cut, no breathing, music overlay, async sound, anxiety as rhythm, Safdie-editing |
| 叙事 | 赌徒即命运+每分钟即生死+成瘾即紧迫=时间即敌人 | gambler as fate, every minute life/death, addiction as urgency, time as enemy, Safdie-narrative |

### 1.2 拉片精华

**Uncut Gems (2019)**: 紧贴面部+不切+珠宝+赌=焦虑即不可呼吸; 声音叠加(多个对话同时)=焦虑即听觉; 结尾突然静止=死亡即沉默
AI适配: `tight on face, no cuts, jewelry, gambling, anxiety as suffocation, sound overlay, sudden silence, Safdie-UncutGems`

**Good Time (2017)**: 霓虹+夜+跑+兄弟=爱即逃亡; 每个场景比上一个更糟=下沉即叙事
AI适配: `neon, night, running, brother, love as escape, each scene worse, sinking as narrative, Safdie-GoodTime`

### 1.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Safdie适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| 赌徒骑士 | 紧贴面部+不切+宝石+焦虑 | tight on face, no cuts, gems, anxiety, gambler knight, Safdie-style |
| 逃亡的紧迫 | 霓虹+夜+跑+每场景更糟 | neon, night, running, each scene worse, Safdie-GoodTime |

## 2. Greta Gerwig — 青春的温度(Lady Bird/Little Women/Barbie)

### 2.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 手持+漂移+快速横摇+稳定器环绕 | handheld, drift, whip pan, Steadicam orbit, Gerwig-style |
| 构图 | 家庭空间+餐桌+舞蹈+框架=成长即家庭 | family space, dining table, dance, framed, growth as family, Gerwig-grade |
| 调色 | 暖色+饱和+复古+季节色=青春即颜色 | warm, saturated, vintage, seasonal, youth as color, Gerwig-palette |
| 光线 | 自然光+窗光+柔散+黄昏=青春即黄昏 | natural, window, soft diffusion, dusk, youth as dusk, Gerwig-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 快速切+匹配剪辑+季节跳切=成长即时间跳跃 | fast cut, match cut, seasonal jump, growth as time jump, Gerwig-editing |
| 叙事 | 成长即离家+家庭即根基+女性即叙事=青春即反叛 | growth as leaving, family as root, women as narrative, youth as rebellion, Gerwig-narrative |

### 2.2 拉片精华

**Lady Bird (2017)**: 萨克拉门托暖+家庭+飞机=离开即成长; 匹配剪辑(家→纽约)=成长即距离
AI适配: `Sacramento warm, family, airplane, leaving as growth, match cut home to NYC, Gerwig-LadyBird`

**Little Women (2019)**: 双时间线(过去暖/现在冷)=成长即褪色; 季节跳切=时间即季节
AI适配: `dual timeline past warm/present cold, growth as fading, seasonal jump cuts, Gerwig-LittleWomen`

**Barbie (2023)**: 粉色+人造+真实vs幻想=女性即选择; 从塑料世界→真实世界=觉醒即褪色
AI适配: `pink, artificial, real vs fantasy, women as choice, plastic to real, awakening as desaturation, Gerwig-Barbie`

## 3. Lynne Ramsay — 暴力的余波(You Were Never Really Here/Ratcatcher)

### 3.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 极慢推入+手持+主观变形+跳切+闪回碎片 | extremely slow push-in, handheld, subjective distortion, jump cut, flashback fragments, Ramsay-style |
| 构图 | 面部特写+极端构图+负空间+日常中的暴力痕迹 | face close-up, extreme composition, negative space, violence traces in everyday, Ramsay-grade |
| 调色 | 去饱和+冷蓝+唯一暖(回忆)=创伤即冷 | desaturated, cold blue, only warm in memory, trauma as cold, Ramsay-palette |
| 光线 | 自然光+过曝+底光+闪回柔焦=创伤即光断裂 | natural, overexposed, underlight, flashback soft, trauma as fractured light, Ramsay-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 碎片闪回+声画不同步+跳切=创伤即碎片 | fragment flashback, async sound, jump cut, trauma as fragments, Ramsay-editing |
| 叙事 | 暴力不展示只看余波+创伤即日常+孩子视角=暴力即沉默 | violence aftermath not shown, trauma as everyday, child POV, violence as silence, Ramsay-narrative |

### 3.2 拉片精华

**You Were Never Really Here (2017)**: 不展示暴力只看余波=暴力即沉默; 闪回碎片(童年/战场)=创伤即碎片; 铁锤=工具即武器
AI适配: `violence aftermath not shown, flashback fragments, hammer as tool/weapon, trauma as fragments, Ramsay-YouWereNever`

**Ratcatcher (1999)**: 孩子+运河+泥+日常=贫穷即日常; 运河=死亡即日常
AI适配: `child, canal, mud, everyday, poverty as everyday, canal as death, Ramsay-Ratcatcher`

**Morvern Callar (2002)**: 男友尸体+音乐+出走=悲伤即出走; 闪光灯+舞池=悲伤即舞
AI适配: `boyfriend body, music, leaving, grief as departure, strobe dance floor, Ramsay-Morvern`

### 3.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Ramsay适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| 暴力的余波(不展示暴力) | 闪回碎片+余波+创伤即碎片 | flashback fragments, aftermath, trauma as fragments, Ramsay-style |
| 孩子眼中的战争 | 孩子+泥+日常+死亡即日常 | child, mud, everyday, death as everyday, Ramsay-Ratcatcher |
| 悲伤的出走 | 尸体+音乐+闪光+悲伤即舞 | body, music, strobe, grief as dance, Ramsay-Morvern |

## 4. Fincher/Nolan/Villeneuve 深度拉片补充

### 4.1 Fincher 补充

**Mank (2020)**: 黑白+1940s好莱坞+深焦+窗光=旧好莱坞即黄金; 闪回结构=剧本即回忆
AI适配: `B&W, 1940s Hollywood, deep focus, window light, golden age as memory, Fincher-Mank`

**The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo (2011)**: 雪+冷蓝+暴力+锁定=北欧即冷酷; 开场credit熔岩=愤怒即熔岩
AI适配: `snow, cold blue, violence, locked-off, Nordic as cold, lava credit sequence, Fincher-DragonTattoo`

**The Social Network (2010)**: 冷蓝+荧光+快速对话+交叉剪辑=代码即权力; 赛艇=竞争即体力
AI适配: `cold blue, fluorescent, fast dialogue, cross-cutting, code as power, rowing as competition, Fincher-SocialNetwork`

### 4.2 Nolan 补充

**Inception (2010)**: 折叠城市+重力翻转=梦即物理; 踢(kick)=梦醒机制; 多层梦同步剪辑=时间即层级
AI适配: `folding city, gravity flip, dream as physics, kick mechanism, multi-layer sync edit, Nolan-Inception`

**Interstellar (2014)**: 巨浪+时间膨胀+书架=爱即维度; IMAX+科学=科幻即科学
AI适配: `giant wave, time dilation, bookshelf, love as dimension, IMAX science, Nolan-Interstellar`

**Tenet (2020)**: 时间逆转+红蓝标记=时间即方向; 正逆同框=因果即选择
AI适配: `time inversion, red/blue marks, forward and reverse same frame, causality as choice, Nolan-Tenet`

### 4.3 Villeneuve 补充

**Prisoners (2013)**: 雨+灰+慢推+沉默=失踪即沉默; 蜡烛+迷宫=寻找即迷宫
AI适配: `rain, grey, slow push, silence, missing as silence, candle maze, Villeneuve-Prisoners`

**Enemy (2013)**: 蜘蛛+分身+黄绿=身份即恐惧; 城市+蜘蛛=城市即网
AI适配: `spider, doppelganger, yellow-green, identity as fear, city as web, Villeneuve-Enemy`

## 6. 具体影片拉片

|------|------|------|------|-----------|
| 开场 | ECU | 结肠内→宝石→人 | 极近→远/内→外=焦虑从内 | ECU inside→gem→person, extreme close→wide, anxiety from within, Safdie |
| 赌注 | MS | 赌桌/汗/紧张/手表 | 手持紧贴/时间=焦虑 | gambling table, sweat, tension, watch, handheld close, time=anxiety, Safdie |
| 耳鸣 | CU | 面部/环境模糊/耳鸣声 | 浅景深+耳鸣=沉浸焦虑 | face CU, environment blurred, tinnitus, shallow DOF, immersive anxiety, Safdie |
