## 1. Darren Aronofsky — 成瘾的螺旋

### 1.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 急推+分屏+时间lapse+蛇眼(SnorrCam)+跳切 | crash zoom, split screen, time-lapse, SnorrCam, jump cut, Aronofsky-style |
| 构图 | 分屏(并置成瘾/现实)+蛇眼(主观迷失)+面部特写极端 | split screen juxtaposition, SnorrCam POV, extreme face close-up, Aronofsky-grade |
| 调色 | 去饱和+冷绿+唯一暖=成瘾的假暖+黑白闪前 | desaturated, cold green, only warm is false, B&W flashforward, Aronofsky-palette |
| 光线 | 顶光+蛇眼光+医院荧光+舞台聚光=体制即暴力 | overhead, SnorrCam light, hospital fluorescent, stage spotlight, system as violence, Aronofsky-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 时间lapse蒙太奇(快进成瘾过程)+跳切+匹配剪辑 | time-lapse addiction montage, jump cut, match cut, Aronofsky-editing |
| 叙事 | 成瘾即螺旋+追求完美即自毁+欲望即黑洞 | addiction as spiral, perfection as self-destruction, desire as black hole, Aronofsky-narrative |

### 1.2 拉片精华

**Requiem for a Dream (2000)**: 分屏(两人同时成瘾)+时间lapse(快进堕落)+蛇眼(迷失主观)=成瘾即物理; 夏天→秋天→冬天=堕落即季节
AI适配: `split screen dual addiction, time-lapse decay, SnorrCam, seasons as decay, Aronofsky-Requiem`

**Black Swan (2010)**: 镜子+分身+急推=完美即疯狂; 白天鹅→黑天鹅=身份即分裂; 镜中另一个自己=自我即敌人
AI适配: `mirrors, doppelganger, crash zoom, perfection as madness, white to black swan, self as enemy, Aronofsky-BlackSwan`

**The Wrestler (2008)**: 手持+16mm粗糙+纪录片感=身体即战场; 每次出场=身体即货币
AI适配: `handheld, 16mm rough, documentary feel, body as battlefield, body as currency, Aronofsky-Wrestler`

**Pi (1998)**: 黑白+粗糙+数学+偏执=数字即疯狂
AI适配: `B&W, rough, mathematics, paranoia, numbers as madness, Aronofsky-Pi`

**Mother! (2017)**: 单房间+越来越多的人+圣经隐喻=创造即毁灭
AI适配: `single room, increasing people, biblical metaphor, creation as destruction, Aronofsky-Mother`

### 1.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Aronofsky适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| 魔法成瘾 | 分屏+时间lapse+蛇眼+季节堕落 | split screen, time-lapse, SnorrCam, magic addiction spiral, Aronofsky-style |
| 完美即疯狂 | 镜子+分身+急推+白→黑 | mirrors, doppelganger, crash zoom, white to black, Aronofsky-BlackSwan |
| 魔法的身体代价 | 手持粗糙+纪录片感+身体即战场 | handheld rough, documentary, body as battlefield, Aronofsky-Wrestler |

## 2. Michael Haneke — 冷酷的观察者

### 2.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 锁定+极慢推入+不手持+长镜头+不干预 | locked-off, extremely slow push-in, no handheld, long take, non-intervention, Haneke-style |
| 构图 | 居中+框架+深景深+日常空间=暴力在日常中 | centered, framed, deep focus, everyday space, violence in the everyday, Haneke-grade |
| 调色 | 去饱和+冷蓝+白墙+医院=体制即冰冷 | desaturated, cold blue, white walls, hospital, system as cold, Haneke-palette |
| 光线 | 均匀照明+荧光灯+无阴影=暴力无阴影可藏 | even illumination, fluorescent, no shadows, violence has no shadow to hide, Haneke-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 极少剪辑+长镜头+硬切+不配乐=沉默即暴力 | minimal cuts, long takes, hard cuts, no score, silence as violence, Haneke-editing |
| 叙事 | 观众即共犯+暴力即日常+不解释+不救赎=你也是加害者 | viewer as accomplice, violence as everyday, no explanation, no redemption, you are also guilty, Haneke-narrative |

### 2.2 拉片精华

**Funny Games (1997/2007)**: 锁定+长镜头+不配乐+打破第四面墙=观众即共犯; 快退/重放=导演干预叙事=观众想看暴力但不想负责
AI适配: `locked-off, no score, breaking fourth wall, viewer as accomplice, rewind/replay, Haneke-FunnyGames`

**Amour (2012)**: 公寓+长镜头+衰老+沉默=爱即忍耐; 固定机位不干预=死亡即日常
AI适配: `apartment, long take, aging, silence, love as endurance, fixed camera non-intervention, Haneke-Amour`

**Caché/Hidden (2005)**: 录像带+谁在看=监视即暴力; 不解答=观众即偷窥者
AI适配: `videotape, who is watching, surveillance as violence, no resolution, viewer as voyeur, Haneke-Cache`

**The White Ribbon (2009)**: 黑白+方形画幅+儿童+社区=邪恶即制度; 不解答=集体即沉默
AI适配: `B&W, square aspect, children, community, evil as system, no resolution, collective silence, Haneke-WhiteRibbon`

### 2.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Haneke适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| 教会的暴力(不展示) | 锁定+长镜头+不配乐+观众共犯 | locked-off, no score, viewer as accomplice, institutional violence, Haneke-style |
| 魔法的代价(衰老) | 公寓+固定机位+沉默+爱即忍耐 | apartment, fixed camera, silence, love as endurance, Haneke-Amour |
| 被监视/被偷窥 | 录像带+谁在看+不解答 | videotape, who is watching, no resolution, surveillance, Haneke-Cache |

## 3. Gaspar Noe — 不可逆的暴力

### 3.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 旋转+漂浮+蛇眼+一镜到底+倒退叙事 | spinning, floating, SnorrCam, one-take, reverse chronology, Noe-style |
| 构图 | 地面仰视+走廊+红/黑+极端空间 | ground level仰, corridor, red/black, extreme space, Noe-grade |
| 调色 | 红色主导+深黑+霓虹+血液=暴力即红色 | red dominant, deep black, neon, blood, violence as red, Noe-palette |
| 光线 | 频闪+红光+霓虹+底光=眩晕即暴力 | strobe, red light, neon, underlighting, vertigo as violence, Noe-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 倒退叙事(不可逆)+黑屏间歇+频闪切换 | reverse chronology, black screen intervals, strobe cuts, Noe-editing |
| 叙事 | 时间不可逆+暴力即永痕+主观即全部=你无法撤回 | time irreversible, violence as permanent mark, subjective is all, you cannot undo, Noe-narrative |

### 3.2 拉片精华

**Irreversible (2002)**: 倒退叙事=先看结果再看起因=你无法撤回; 旋转+蛇眼=迷失=暴力即眩晕; 红色走廊隧道=地狱
AI适配: `reverse chronology, spinning SnorrCam, red tunnel corridor, you cannot undo, violence as vertigo, Noe-Irreversible`

**Enter the Void (2009)**: 第一人称+漂浮+DMT幻觉+霓虹东京=死亡即飞翔; 开场DMT=意识即电影
AI适配: `first person, floating, DMT hallucination, neon Tokyo, death as flying, Noe-EnterTheVoid`

**Climax (2018)**: 一镜到底+舞蹈→疯狂+倒挂天花板=集体即疯狂; 频闪+红=舞池即地狱
AI适配: `one-take, dance to madness, upside-down ceiling, collective madness, strobe red, dance floor as hell, Noe-Climax`

### 3.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Noe适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|--------|--------|
| 魔法暴力的不可逆 | 倒退叙事+旋转+红走廊 | reverse chronology, spinning, red corridor, irreversible magic violence, Noe-style |
| 死亡/灵魂出窍 | 第一人称漂浮+霓虹+飞翔 | first person floating, neon, death as flying, Noe-EnterTheVoid |
| 集体疯狂/仪式失控 | 一镜到底+频闪+倒挂 | one-take, strobe, upside-down, collective madness, Noe-Climax |
