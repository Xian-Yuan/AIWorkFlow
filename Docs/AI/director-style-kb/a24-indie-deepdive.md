## 1. A24厂牌美学体系(深化)

### 1.1 A24视觉DNA六层

| 层 | 特征 | 具体手法 | 提示词 | 西幻翻译 |
|---|------|---------|--------|---------|
| 色彩 | 去饱和+单色主调+偶尔突变 | desaturated base, monochrome dominant, sudden color burst | A24 palette, desaturated, single dominant hue, restrained, color burst only at climax | 调色板与情绪绑定，突变=魔法/暴力 |
| 构图 | 精确+极简+负空间为主 | precise framing, minimalist, negative space dominant | A24 composition, precise framing, minimalist, breathing room, nothing wasted | 每帧只有必需元素 |
| 运动 | 缓慢/静止/偶尔爆发 | slow/still, occasional burst | A24 movement, slow deliberate, mostly static, sudden violence | 静→暴=恐惧/魔法 |
| 声音 | 低频嗡鸣+极端寂静+不配乐 | low frequency drone, extreme silence, no score | A24 sound design, low drone, silence, no score, ambience only | 环境声代替配乐 |
| 叙事 | 缓慢燃烧+模糊类型+拒绝解释 | slow burn, de-genre, no explanation | A24 narrative, slow burn, ambiguous, no resolution, let audience interpret | 不解释魔法/不解释诅咒 |
| 身体 | 微距+不适+身体即战场 | macro, body horror, physical unease | A24 body, macro close-up, skin, breath, physical unease, visceral | 诅咒从皮肤开始 |
