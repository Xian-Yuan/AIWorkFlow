## 1. Michael Mann — 都市夜景的孤独(Heat/Collateral)

### 1.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 稳定器+手持混合+长焦压缩+缓慢环绕 | Steadicam+handheld mix, telephoto compression, slow orbit, Mann-style |
| 构图 | 长焦压缩(人叠背景)+都市水平线+车内+夜景广角 | telephoto compression, urban horizon, car interior, night wide, Mann-grade |
| 调色 | 蓝夜+橙钠灯+冷白荧光=都市即孤独 | blue night, orange sodium, cold fluorescent, city as loneliness, Mann-palette |
| 光线 | 霓虹+钠灯橙+荧光冷白+机场灯+雨反射=夜晚即城市 | neon, sodium orange, fluorescent white, airport lights, rain reflection, night as city, Mann-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 交叉剪辑(警vs匪)+长镜头对话+快切动作 | cross-cutting cop vs criminal, long take dialogue, fast action cuts, Mann-editing |
| 叙事 | 职业即身份+孤独即都市+面对面即尊重 | professionalism as identity, loneliness as city, face-to-face as respect, Mann-narrative |

### 1.2 拉片精华

**Heat (1995)**: 咖啡店对话(Pacino vs De Niro)=对手即镜像; 夜景+高速路=都市即战场; 长焦压缩=距离即孤独
AI适配: `coffee shop face-off, rival as mirror, night highway, city as battlefield, telephoto compression, distance as loneliness, Mann-Heat`

**Collateral (2004)**: 出租车+夜景+钠灯橙=一夜即旅程; 暖(车内)/冷(外)对切=两个世界
AI适配: `taxi, night, sodium orange, one night journey, warm inside/cold outside, Mann-Collateral`

**Thief (1981)**: 霓虹+雨+钻石+孤独=犯罪即职业; 蓝夜+手电=黑暗中的微光
AI适配: `neon, rain, diamond, loneliness, crime as profession, blue night flashlight, Mann-Thief`

**Miami Vice (2006)**: 水面+快艇+热+霓虹=热带即犯罪; 过曝+柔散=热浪即暴力
AI适配: `water, speedboat, heat, neon, tropical crime, overexposed soft, heat as violence, Mann-MiamiVice`

### 1.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Mann适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|---------|--------|
| 夜城追逐 | 蓝夜+钠灯橙+霓虹+雨反射 | blue night, sodium orange, neon, rain, urban chase, Mann-style |
| 骑士vs暗杀者对峙 | 长焦压缩+车内+交叉剪辑 | telephoto, car interior, cross-cutting, Mann-Heat |
| 地下交易/走私 | 水面+快艇+过曝+霓虹 | water, overexposed, neon, underground trade, Mann-MiamiVice |

## 2. Steven Soderbergh — 冷静的控制(Ocean's/Out of Sight/Traffic)

### 2.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 手持+稳定器+快速横移+分屏+跳切 | handheld, Steadicam, fast lateral, split screen, jump cut, Soderbergh-style |
| 构图 | 分屏(同时多空间)+框架+冷色调+几何 | split screen, framed, cool tones, geometric, Soderbergh-grade |
| 调色 | 冷蓝绿+去饱和+不同色调标记不同世界 | cool teal, desaturated, different color grade per world, Soderbergh-palette |
| 光线 | 自然光+荧光+霓虹+低调=职业即冷静 | natural, fluorescent, neon, low key, professionalism as calm, Soderbergh-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 分屏+闪回穿插+时间跳跃+快速蒙太奇 | split screen, flashback intercut, time jump, fast montage, Soderbergh-editing |
| 叙事 | 职业即艺术+抢劫即编舞+多线同步=犯罪即精确 | professionalism as art, heist as choreography, multi-thread sync, crime as precision, Soderbergh-narrative |

### 2.2 拉片精华

**Ocean's Eleven (2001)**: 分屏+快速蒙太奇+冷蓝绿=抢劫即编舞; 闪光+慢动作=高潮即仪式
AI适配: `split screen, fast montage, cool teal, heist as choreography, flash slow-mo, Soderbergh-Oceans`

**Traffic (2000)**: 三线三色调(蓝=DC/黄=墨西哥/冷=圣地亚哥)=世界即色调; 手持+跳切=纪录片感
AI适配: `three threads three color grades, blue DC/yellow Mexico/cold San Diego, handheld, Soderbergh-Traffic`

**Out of Sight (1998)**: 闪回穿插+冷调+调情即犯罪=欲望即风险
AI适配: `flashback intercut, cool tones, flirting as crime, desire as risk, Soderbergh-OutOfSight`

### 2.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Soderbergh适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|---------------|--------|
| 魔法抢劫/盗宝 | 分屏+快速蒙太奇+冷蓝绿 | split screen, fast montage, cool teal, heist as choreography, Soderbergh-style |
| 多线阴谋(色调编码) | 三线三色调+手持+跳切 | three threads three colors, handheld, Soderbergh-Traffic |
| 禁忌调情 | 闪回穿插+冷调+欲望即风险 | flashback intercut, cool, desire as risk, Soderbergh-OutOfSight |

## 3. Martin Scorsese — 深度补充(补充crime-thriller已有条目)

### 3.1 补充签名(与Fincher/Trier区分)

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 长镜头跟拍(Goodfellas餐厅)=权力即入场; 快速横摇=时间跳跃 | long take follow Goodfellas, power as entrance, whip pan time jump, Scorsese-style |
| 构图 | 慢动作(入场)+快切(暴力)+定格(标志性) | slow-mo entrance, fast cut violence, freeze frame signature, Scorsese-grade |
| 剪辑 | 快速剪辑+音乐驱动+旁白驱动=节奏来自音乐 | fast cut, music-driven, voiceover-driven, rhythm from music, Scorsese-editing |
| 叙事 | 崛起即坠落+暴力即日常+背叛即代价=美国梦即噩梦 | rise and fall, violence as everyday, betrayal as cost, American dream as nightmare, Scorsese-narrative |

### 3.2 补充拉片

**Goodfellas (1990)**: 餐厅长镜头跟拍(后门→走廊→餐桌)=权力即入场; 定格"我一辈子想当黑帮"=美国梦即黑帮梦
AI适配: `restaurant long take follow back door to table, power as entrance, freeze frame, Scorsese-Goodfellas`

**The Departed (2006)**: 交叉剪辑(卧底vs内鬼)=谁是谁; 手机=信息即权力; 电梯=命运即垂直
AI适配: `cross-cutting mole vs rat, phone as power, elevator as fate vertical, Scorsese-Departed`

**Taxi Driver (1976)**: 雨中霓虹+车内孤独+慢推面部=城市即孤独; "你在跟我说话?"=疯狂即镜子
AI适配: `rain neon, car interior loneliness, slow push face, city as isolation, mirror monologue, Scorsese-TaxiDriver`

**Raging Bull (1980)**: 黑白+拳击+慢动作=暴力即自我毁灭; 绳圈内的空间=暴力即牢笼
AI适配: `B&W, boxing, slow motion, violence as self-destruction, ring as cage, Scorsese-RagingBull`

### 3.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Scorsese适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| 领主入场的权力 | 长镜头跟拍+慢动作+音乐驱动 | long take follow, slow-mo, music-driven, power entrance, Scorsese-Goodfellas |
| 双面间谍/卧底 | 交叉剪辑+手机+电梯=命运垂直 | cross-cutting, phone, elevator, fate vertical, Scorsese-Departed |
| 孤独骑士的疯狂 | 雨中霓虹+车内+慢推面部 | rain neon, car interior, slow push face, Scorsese-TaxiDriver |

## 4. 都市黑色深度场景拉片

> 补充3位导演的关键场景深度拉片+西幻翻译

### 4.1 Michael Mann — 关键场景拉片

**场景A: Heat — 咖啡店对峙(Pacino vs De Niro)**

> 这是电影史上最经典的"两个世界的人面对面"场景

| 镜号 | 景别 | 运镜 | 光线 | 调色 | 提示词 | 西幻翻译 |
|------|------|------|------|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | MS 两人对面坐 | 锁定+缓慢 | 钠灯橙+暗 | 深蓝夜+钠灯橙 | `MS two men across table, locked slow, sodium orange dark, deep blue night, Mann-Heat` | 审判官vs被审判者 |
| 2 | CU Pacino | 极慢推入 | 侧光+钠灯 | 暖侧+冷背 | `CU Pacino, extreme slow push, side sodium, warm side cold back, Mann-close-up` | 审判者的凝视 |
| 3 | CU De Niro | 极慢推入 | 侧光+钠灯 | 暖侧+冷背 | `CU De Niro, extreme slow push, side sodium, warm side cold back, Mann-close-up` | 被审者的沉默 |
| 4 | MS 两人正面 | 锁定 | 双钠灯 | 双暖+中暗 | `MS two frontal, locked, dual sodium, dual warm center dark, Mann-face-off` | 两个世界的交汇 |
| 5 | WS 咖啡店外夜景 | 缓慢拉出 | 霓虹+车灯 | 深蓝+橙 | `WS diner exterior night, slow pull-out, neon headlights, deep blue orange, Mann-city` | 回到各自的孤独 |

**Mann咖啡店对峙→西幻翻译**:
`diner face-off → tavern face-off, knight and outlaw across table, sodium orange → torchlight warm, Mann-fantasy-confrontation`

**场景B: Collateral — 夜间出租车(关键视觉)**

| 元素 | 特征 | 英文提示词 | 西幻翻译 |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| 出租车内 | 钠灯橙+仪表蓝+人脸暖冷对分 | taxi interior, sodium orange dash blue, face warm-cold split, Collateral-taxi | 马车内/灯笼橙+月光蓝 |
| 夜间城市 | 深蓝+钠灯橙+雨+霓虹 | deep blue, sodium orange, rain, neon, Collateral-city | 夜间魔法城市/黑市 |
| 暗杀 | 冷静+锁定+一击+暗处 | calm locked, one strike, from dark, Collateral-hit | 暗杀/刺客/冷血杀手 |

**Mann夜景美学核心**: 城市的蓝夜+钠灯橙=孤独的温暖
```
"Mann-night-city, deep blue night, sodium-vapor orange, 
rain and neon, taxi interior warm-cold split, 
lonely professional, urban noir isolation"
```

### 4.2 Steven Soderbergh — 关键场景拉片

**场景: Ocean's Eleven — 赌场抢劫编舞**

| 元素 | 特征 | 英文提示词 | 西幻翻译 |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| 分屏 | 同时展示多个行动 | split screen, simultaneous actions, Ocean's-split | 多人同时施法/多人分头行动 |
| 快速蒙太奇 | 编舞式信息密集 | rapid montage, choreographed information, Ocean's-montage | 计划展示/战术编排 |
| 冷蓝绿 | 赌场冷色调 | cold blue-green, casino cold, Ocean's-grade | 魔法建筑冷光/地牢蓝绿 |
| 精确轨道 | 每个运动精确计算 | precise track, calculated motion, Ocean's-camera | 仪式精确/法阵编舞 |

**Soderbergh分屏→西幻翻译**:
`split screen robbery → split screen spell-casting, multiple mages simultaneous ritual, Ocean's-fantasy-heist`

### 4.3 Martin Scorsese — 关键场景拉片

**场景A: Goodfellas — Copacabana长镜头跟拍**

> 3分钟不切+跟拍+穿过后厨→进入餐厅=权力的入场

| 时间 | 景别 | 运镜 | 光线 | 调色 | 提示词 |
|------|------|------|------|------|--------|
| 0:00 | FS 两人走入后门 | Steadicam跟拍 | 走廊暗光 | 暖暗 | `FS entering back door, Steadicam follow, corridor dim, warm dark, Goodfellas-entry` |
| 0:30 | MS 穿过后厨 | 跟拍+环绕 | 厨房暖光 | 暖+蒸汽 | `MS through kitchen, follow orbit, kitchen warm, steam, Goodfellas-kitchen` |
| 1:30 | FS 走入餐厅 | 跟拍+推开人群 | 餐厅暖光 | 暖+人群 | `FS entering restaurant, follow push through crowd, warm, Goodfellas-arrival` |
| 2:30 | MS 坐下+桌前特写 | 锁定+服务员上前 | 桌光+暗背 | 暖面+暗背 | `MS sitting, locked, waiter approaches, table light dark back, Goodfellas-power` |

**Scorsese长镜跟拍→西幻翻译**:
`Copacabana entry → castle great hall entry, knight walks through corridors to throne, Steadicam follow, power arrival, Goodfellas-fantasy`

**场景B: The Departed — 对峙场景**

| 元素 | 特征 | 英文提示词 | 西幻翻译 |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| 屋顶 | 天台+冷光+风+城市背景 | rooftop, cold light, wind, city backdrop, Departed-rooftop | 城墙/塔顶/冷风 |
| 对峙 | 极近+手持+不切+两枪互指 | extreme close, handheld, no cut, two guns pointed, Departed-confrontation | 两人对峙/拔剑互指 |
| 突然暴力 | 不预警+一声+倒下 | no warning, one shot, falls, Departed-sudden | 突然暴力/不预警 |
| 背叛 | 始终有人从背后来 | always someone from behind, betrayal mechanics, Departed-betrayal | 背叛/阴谋/暗杀 |

**Scorsese暴力美学核心**: 突然+不预警+暴力即日常
```
"Scorsese-violence, sudden no warning, violence is daily, 
Steadicam follow power arrival, rooftop cold wind confrontation, 
betrayal from behind, Goodfellas-Departed-grade"
```
