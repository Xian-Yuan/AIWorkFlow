## 1. Pan's Labyrinth (Guillermo del Toro, 2006) — 童话即恐怖

### 1.1 整体签名

| 维度 | 特征 | AI提示词 |
|------|------|---------|
| 调色 | 琥珀暖(童话)+冷蓝灰(现实)=双重世界 | amber warm(fairy)+cold blue-grey(reality), dual world, delToro-Pan |
| 构图 | 地下=居中+对称+生物美; 地上=偏轴+手持+灰 | underground: centered+symmetric+creature beauty; above: off-axis+handheld+grey |
| 叙事 | 童话≠安全=怪物有悲悯+人有怪物 | fairy tale≠safe, monsters have empathy, humans are monsters, Pan-narrative |

### 1.2 关键场景拆解

**场景A: 首次进入迷宫(0:11:00)**

| 要素 | 细节 | AI提示词 |
|------|------|---------|
| 运镜 | 跟随Ofelia→下降→老树根→石阶→缓慢推入 | follow Ofelia→descend→old roots→stone steps→slow push-in, Pan-descent |
| 构图 | 垂直纵深+根+石+绿暗+唯一暖(灯笼) | vertical depth, roots, stone, green-dark, sole warm(lantern), Pan-labyrinth-entry |
| 生物 | 藤蔓+眼+缓慢+古老+不威胁 | vines, eyes, slow, ancient, non-threatening, Pan-faun-first |
| 情绪 | 好奇+敬畏+不安 | curiosity+awe+unease, Pan-entry-emotion |

**AI短剧适配**: 进入魔法地下/迷宫: `follow character→descend→ancient roots→stone steps→slow push-in, vertical depth, green-dark, sole warm lantern, ancient creature, Pan-labyrinth-entry medieval`

**场景B: 苍白人(Pale Man)宴席(0:53:00)**

| 要素 | 细节 | AI提示词 |
|------|------|---------|
| 构图 | 对称+长桌+食物+苍白+眼在手上 | symmetric, long table, food, pale, eyes on hands, Pan-Pale-Man |
| 运镜 | 极慢推入+锁定+不切+Ofelia视角 | extremely slow push-in, locked, no cut, Ofelia POV, Pan-Pale-tension |
| 声音 | 极轻+呼吸+心跳+突然→追逐 | very quiet, breathing, heartbeat, sudden→chase, Pan-Pale-sound |
| 色彩 | 红(食物)+苍白+暗=欲望即危险 | red(food), pale, dark, desire as danger, Pan-Pale-color |

**AI短剧适配**: 诅咒宴席/不可吃的食物: `symmetric long table, forbidden food, pale creature with eyes on hands, extremely slow push-in, red accent, desire as danger, Pan-Pale-Man medieval cursed feast`
