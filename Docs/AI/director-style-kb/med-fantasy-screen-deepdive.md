## 1. The Green Knight (2021) — David Lowery

> DP: Joe Anderson | 美术指导: Jade Healy | 调色: Joe Walker
> 核心美学: 亚瑟王传说的民间故事版——不是金属铠甲的史诗，是苔藓覆盖的童话

### 1.1 视觉签名

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 | 情绪内核 |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| 调色 | 自然的绿+泥土棕+唯一红色=魔法侵入 | natural green, earth brown, singular red intrusion, Lowery-grade, Green-Knight-palette | 自然中的超自然 |
| 光线 | 自然光为主+突然体积光=神圣时刻 | natural ambient, sudden volumetric shaft = divine intervention | 日常→神圣的跃迁 |
| 构图 | 居中对称(仪式感)+偏轴失重(迷失) | centered ritual framing, off-axis disorientation, Lowery-composition | 秩序与迷茫并存 |
| 景别 | 极远景(自然压倒人物)+极近景(肉体感知) | extreme wide nature dominates + ECU body awareness | 人与自然的力量差 |
| 运镜 | 缓慢漂移+偶尔固定凝视 | slow drifting camera, occasional locked contemplation | 梦游般的节奏 |
| 服装 | 粗麻+泥土+非英雄化 | coarse linen, earth-toned, unheroic garb, non-glamorous medieval | 反英雄化中世纪 |

### 1.2 关键场景拉片

**场景: Gawain与Green Knight初次遭遇(圣诞绿色骑士闯入)**

| 镜号 | 景别 | 运镜 | 光线 | 调色 | 提示词 |
|------|------|------|------|------|--------|
| 1 | WS 大厅全景 | 锁定/居中对称 | 暖火光+门口冷风 | 暖橙大厅 | `wide shot, centered symmetrical great hall, warm firelight, festive, Green Knight` |
| 2 | MS 骑士进门 | 缓慢横摇 | 门口逆光剪影 | 暖内→冷外分界 | `medium shot, figure in doorway backlit, warm interior cold exterior, threshold moment` |
| 3 | CU 骑士面部 | 锁定 | 绿色环境光从下方 | 全画面绿光笼罩 | `close-up, green environmental glow from below, otherworldly presence, Green Knight face` |
| 4 | ECU 骑士眼部 | 锁定 | 纯绿色光 | 绿色吞噬 | `extreme close-up, pure green glow in eyes, supernatural force, inhuman gaze` |
| 5 | WS 全厅反应 | 缓慢拉出 | 火光暗淡/绿光渐强 | 暖→绿渐变 | `wide, pulling out, firelight dimming, green light growing, nature overtaking civilization` |

**场景: Gawain穿越森林(旅途迷失)**

| 镜号 | 景别 | 运镜 | 光线 | 调色 | 提示词 |
|------|------|------|------|------|--------|
| 1 | EWS 森林全景 | 锁定/负空间 | 雾+漫射 | 去饱和绿灰 | `extreme wide, forest in fog, desaturated green-grey, tiny figure, nature dominates` |
| 2 | FS Gawain行走 | 缓慢跟随 | 树冠漏光 | 自然绿+唯一暖光 | `full shot, following, canopy light, natural green, sole warm point on figure` |
| 3 | CU 脚下枯叶 | 低角度固定 | 斑驳地面光 | 泥土棕 | `close-up, feet on dead leaves, low angle, dappled ground light, earth tones` |
| 4 | MS 雾中模糊身影 | 锁定/前景遮挡 | 雾化一切 | 绿灰消融 | `medium shot, figure blurred in fog, foreground branch obstruction, green-grey dissolution` |

slow drift, extreme wide nature dominates, unheroic coarse linen, 
natural ambient with sudden volumetric, fog and moss, Green-Knight-grade"

## 2. The Northman (2022) — Robert Eggers

> DP: Jarin Blaschke | 美术指导: Craig Lathrop | 调色: Skip Kimball
> 核心美学: 考据极端主义——9世纪维京的每一帧都是考古现场

### 2.1 视觉签名

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 | 情绪内核 |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| 画幅 | 近方形2.00:1(非宽银幕) | near-square 2.00:1 aspect, claustrophobic framing, Eggers-aspect | 窒息/困困/无处可逃 |
| 光线 | 仅实用光源(火把/壁炉/日光) | practical light only, torch fire, hearth glow, no fill, Eggers-lighting | 真实黑暗=无灯即不可见 |
| 调色 | 去饱和+冷灰+唯一暖=火 | desaturated, cold grey, sole warmth = firelight, Eggers-grade | 压迫中唯一生存温度 |
| 运镜 | 极少切/长镜头/一镜到底(角斗) | minimal cuts, long takes, one-take combat, Eggers-editing | 时间不可切/暴力不可逃 |
| 构图 | 低角度仰视(神灵)+俯视(牺牲) | low angle worship, high angle sacrifice, ritual framing | 神圣vs献祭的等级 |
| 服装 | 考据精确+动物皮毛+泥土 | historically accurate, animal pelt, mud-encrusted, Eggers-costume | 真实粗糙/绝非英雄化 |

| 2 | MS 船首Amleth | 缓慢推入 | 侧光/火把 | 冷蓝+唯一火光 | `medium, Amleth at prow, slow push-in, side torch light, cold blue sole warm fire` |

## 3. Nosferatu (2024) — Robert Eggers

> DP: Jarin Blaschke | 美术指导: Craig Lathrop
> 核心美学: 19世纪哥特+吸血鬼民间传说——不是浪漫，是瘟疫和死亡

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 | 情绪内核 |
|------|------|-----------|---------|
| 画幅 | 可能延续2.00:1或更窄 | near-square, claustrophobic, Gothic-aspect | 幽闭恐惧 |
| 光线 | 仅烛光+壁炉+月光 | candlelight only, hearth fire, moonlight, no ambient fill, Nosferatu-lighting | 黑暗中仅有人造光 |
| 调色 | 去饱和+冷蓝灰+唯一暖=烛 | desaturated cold blue-grey, sole warmth is candlelight, Nosferatu-grade | 瘟疫笼罩/死亡渗透 |
| 阴影 | 阴影即角色——Orlok的阴影独立行动 | shadow as character, independent shadow movement, expressionist shadow play | 哥特表现主义传承 |
| 构图 | 对称走廊+纵深=吸血鬼不可逃 | symmetric corridor, one-point perspective, inescapable vampire presence | 命运走廊 |
| 运镜 | 极慢推入+突然手持=瘟疫侵入 | extremely slow push-in, sudden handheld = plague intrusion | 缓慢恐怖+突然崩溃 |

### 3.2 Nosferatu美学速用公式
