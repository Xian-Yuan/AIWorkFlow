## 1. Werner Herzog — 狂喜的真实(Grizzly Man / Fitzcarraldo / Cave of Forgotten Dreams)

### 1.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 锁定远景+缓慢升降+不干预+极端位置 | locked wide, slow crane, non-intervention, extreme position, Herzog-style |
| 构图 | 自然占80%+人占20%+垂直纵深+地平线极低 | nature 80% human 20%, vertical depth, very low horizon, Herzog-grade |
| 调色 | 自然色+去饱和+唯一暖(火/光)=文明即入侵 | natural, desaturated, sole warm(fire/light), civilization as intrusion, Herzog-palette |
| 光线 | 自然光+黄金时段+极地光+洞穴火把=自然即上帝 | natural light, golden hour, arctic light, cave torch, nature as god, Herzog-lighting |
| 声音 | 极简旁白+自然声+沉默=叙述即沉思 | minimal voiceover, natural sound, silence, narration as meditation, Herzog-sound |
| 叙事 | 狂喜的真实+极端自然+人的渺小+疯子即圣人 | ecstatic truth, extreme nature, human insignificance, madman as saint, Herzog-narrative |

### 1.2 核心作品拉片

**Grizzly Man (2005)**: 被熊吃的人=自然即无情+影像=遗物+旁白=沉思+边界即死亡
AI适配: `nature as merciless, footage as relic, voiceover as meditation, boundary as death, Herzog-Grizzly`

**Fitzcarraldo (1982)**: 拖船过山=疯子即英雄+丛林即地狱+歌剧=文明即妄想
AI适配: `ship over mountain, madman as hero, jungle as hell, opera as civilization delusion, Herzog-Fitzcarraldo`

**Cave of Forgotten Dreams (2010)**: 3D洞穴壁画=时间即艺术+火把光=祖先+壁画=人类即创造
AI适配: `cave paintings, firelight, ancestors, art as time, human as creator, Herzog-Cave`

**Aguirre Wrath of God (1972)**: 河流=毁灭+疯子=权力+丛林=吞噬+raft=文明残骸
AI适配: `river as destruction, madman as power, jungle as consuming, raft as civilization wreck, Herzog-Aguirre`
