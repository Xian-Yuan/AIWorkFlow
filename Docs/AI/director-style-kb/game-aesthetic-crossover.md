## 1. FromSoftware (宮崎英高) — 废墟叙事与环境讲故事(Dark Souls / Elden Ring / Bloodborne)

### 1.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 低角度仰视建筑+缓慢升降+锁定玩家视角+死亡回放 | low angle仰望architecture, slow crane, locked player POV, death replay, FromSoft-style |
| 构图 | 废墟纵深+巨人尺度对比+单点透视走廊+篝火=唯一安全点 | ruins depth, giant scale contrast, one-point corridor, bonfire as sole safety, Souls-grade |
| 调色 | 去饱和灰绿+灰蓝+唯一暖色(篝火橙)+血红=绝望中唯一温暖 | desaturated grey-green, grey-blue, sole warm (bonfire orange), blood red, warmth in despair, Souls-palette |
| 光线 | 篝火唯一暖源+自然散射+月光冷+阴影吞噬=光明即奢侈 | bonfire as only warm source, natural scatter, cold moonlight, shadow swallows, light as luxury, Souls-lighting |
| 空间 | 垂直纵深(向上=危险)+螺旋上升+捷径连接+迷路即叙事 | vertical depth up=danger, spiral ascent, shortcut connections, getting lost as narrative, Souls-space |
| 叙事 | 环境讲故事+物品描述=历史+碎片叙事+NPC不可靠+绝望即美学 | environmental storytelling, item description as history, fragment narrative, unreliable NPC, despair as aesthetic, Souls-narrative |

### 1.2 核心作品拉片

**Dark Souls (2011)**: 传火=献祭即延续; 篝火=唯一休息; 安德烈铁匠=不变即安慰; 葛温=烧尽即最终Boss
AI适配: `fire linking as sacrifice, bonfire as only rest, Andre as constancy, Gwyn as burned final boss, DarkSouls`

**Dark Souls III (2016)**: 灰烬墓地+枯萎世界+残火=末日即轮回; 持续崩溃=传火即谎言
AI适配: `ash cemetery, withered world, ember, apocalypse as cycle, collapsing world, fire linking as lie, DS3`

**Bloodborne (2015)**: 维多利亚哥特+血月+变形=猎即怪物; 月光=禁忌知识; 哥特城市纵深
AI适配: `Victorian gothic, blood moon, transformation, hunter as monster, moonlight as forbidden knowledge, gothic city depth, Bloodborne`

**Elden Ring (2022)**: 开放世界+黄金树=秩序即衰败; 碎片=权力即混乱; 巨人尺度=渺小即敬畏
AI适配: `open world, Erdtree as order=decay, shards as power=chaos, giant scale, insignificance as awe, EldenRing`

**Sekiro (2019)**: 日本战国+樱花+雪=死亡即美丽; 修罗=暴力即堕落; 傀儡=控制即非人
AI适配: `Sengoku Japan, cherry blossom+snow, death as beauty, Shura as violence=fall, puppet as control=inhuman, Sekiro`

### 1.3 AI短剧适配 — 中世纪西幻场景

| 西幻场景 | FromSoft适配 | 提示词公式 |

## 2. The Legend of Zelda — 冒险的诗学(Breath of the Wild / Tears of the Kingdom / Ocarina of Time)

### 2.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 远景建立+缓慢推入+自然跟随+骑马航拍 | wide establishing, slow push-in, natural follow, horse riding aerial, Zelda-style |
| 构图 | 三分法自然+角色小/自然大+地平线低+天空大比例 | rule of thirds nature, character tiny/landscape huge, low horizon, sky dominant, Zelda-grade |
| 调色 | 吉卜力水彩+自然饱和+黄金时段+绿地蓝水=冒险即自然 | Ghibli watercolor, natural saturation, golden hour, green land blue water, adventure as nature, Zelda-palette |
| 光线 | 黄金时段+自然散射+体积光穿过树+月光魔法 | golden hour, natural scatter, volumetric through trees, moonlight magic, Zelda-lighting |
| 空间 | 开放世界+地标可见=方向即叙事+神庙=谜题+高塔=视野 | open world, landmark visible=direction, shrine=puzzle, tower=vista, Zelda-space |
| 叙事 | 英雄之旅+沉默主角+冒险即成长+自然即老师 | hero journey, silent protagonist, adventure as growth, nature as teacher, Zelda-narrative |

### 2.2 核心作品拉片

**Ocarina of Time (1998)**: 时光穿梭+童年→成年=时间即代价; 海拉鲁平原=自由即广袤; 时之神殿=时间即谜题
AI适配: `time travel, child to adult, time as cost, Hyrule field, freedom as vastness, Temple of Time, OoT`

**Breath of the Wild (2017)**: 开放+废墟+自然恢复=灾难后即重生; 攀爬=自由; 烹饪=生存即创造
AI适配: `open, ruins, nature reclaims, post-disaster rebirth, climbing as freedom, cooking as survival creation, BotW`

**Tears of the Kingdom (2023)**: 天空岛+地下=三层世界+建造=创造即自由; 超手=组合即可能
AI适配: `sky islands, underground, three-layer world, building as freedom, Ultrahand as combination, TotK`

**Majora's Mask (2000)**: 三天循环=时间即末日+面具=身份即变换+月亮=恐惧即逼近
AI适配: `3-day loop, time as doom, mask as identity, moon as approaching fear, Majora`

## 3. Team ICO (上田文人) — 孤独的连接(ICO / Shadow of the Colossus / The Last Guardian)

### 3.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 极慢推入+锁定跟随+远观+呼吸节奏运镜 | extremely slow push-in, locked follow, distant observation, breathing rhythm camera, Ueda-style |
| 构图 | 角色极小/建筑极大+负空间+留白+垂直纵深 | character tiny/building huge, negative space, emptiness, vertical depth, Ueda-grade |
| 调色 | 去饱和土色+灰蓝+唯一暖(手/火把)+沙色=孤独即颜色 | desaturated earth, grey-blue, sole warm (hand/torch), sand, solitude as color, ICO-palette |
| 光线 | 自然散射+日光柱+阴影长+唯一光=连接=希望 | natural scatter, light shaft, long shadows, single light source, connection as hope, ICO-lighting |
| 空间 | 巨大废墟+垂直迷宫+牵手=安全+坠落=恐惧 | massive ruins, vertical maze, hand-holding as safety, falling as fear, ICO-space |
| 叙事 | 无语言叙事+陪伴即故事+牺牲即爱+孤独即力量 | wordless narrative, companionship as story, sacrifice as love, solitude as strength, ICO-narrative |

### 3.2 核心作品拉片

**ICO (2001)**: 牵手=信任+弱即强+光=驱逐暗+城堡=禁锢即逃脱
AI适配: `hand-holding as trust, weak as strong, light banishes dark, castle as imprisonment=escape, ICO`

**Shadow of the Colossus (2005)**: 巨人=敬畏+杀即堕落+荒原=孤独+马=唯一伙伴+每次杀=角色变暗
AI适配: `colossus as awe, killing as corruption, wasteland as solitude, horse as sole companion, each kill darkens hero, SotC`

**The Last Guardian (2016)**: 少年+巨兽=信任即成长+被困=等待+分离=痛苦+重逢=完整
AI适配: `boy+beast, trust as growth, trapped as waiting, separation as pain, reunion as wholeness, TLG`

### 3.3 AI短剧适配 — 中世纪西幻最核心参考

| 西幻场景 | ICO/Team适配 | 提示词公式 |

| 巨龙/魔物(有灵性) | 角色极小+巨人+敬畏+不杀 | `tiny character, giant creature, awe, mercy, Ueda-grade, SotC-colossus` |
| 废墟探索 | 极慢推入+垂直纵深+灰蓝+牵手 | `extremely slow push-in, vertical depth, grey-blue, hand-holding, ICO-ruins` |
