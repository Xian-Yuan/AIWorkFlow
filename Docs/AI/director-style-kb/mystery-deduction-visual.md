## 1. Alfred Hitchcock 深潜 — 悬念即信息差/主观镜头/恐惧即期待

### 1.1 六维度签名(深潜扩展)

| 维度 | 签名特征 | 英文提示词 | 情绪内核 |

| 调色 | Vertigo: 绿+红/冷蓝阴影;Psycho: 黑白极端;Rear Window: 暖内+冷外 | Vertigo: green+red / Psycho: B&W extreme / Rear Window: warm inside+cold outside, Hitchcock palette | 不同恐惧不同色 |

| 叙事 | 无辜者被追/双重身份/冰山金发/母亲阴影 | wrong man pursued, dual identity, ice blonde, mother shadow, Hitchcock narrative | 安全感的崩塌 |

### 1.2 Hitchcock悬念公式

`
Hitchcock Suspense =
  Audience KNOWS danger (bomb under table)
  + Characters DON'T KNOW
  + Camera shows danger approaching
  + Characters continue unaware
  = Maximum audience anxiety
  (NOT: surprise = audience learns when character learns)
`

**提示词**: Hitchcock suspense: audience sees danger, character unaware, camera reveals approaching threat, cross-cut danger and normalcy, information gap = tension

## 5. 推理/悬疑→中世纪西幻通用翻译表

| 推理核心技法 | 语法 | 西幻翻译 | 提示词速配 |
|------------|------|---------|-----------|
| 悬念≠惊奇(Hitchcock) | 信息差=焦虑 | 观众知道诅咒/角色不知道 | Hitchcock suspense, audience knows curse, character unaware, cross-cut |
| 主观镜头(Hitchcock) | 观众=角色 | 主观魔法视角 | Hitchcock POV, subjective magic vision, we see what character sees |

## 7. 推理/悬疑关键场景模板(直出提示词)

### 7.1 Hitchcock式悬念(诅咒追踪)

`
WS: castle corridor, candle-lit, long shadows, one-point perspective, Hitchcock-suspense, 9:16
MS: knight tracking someone, POV shot, we see what he sees, subjective, Hitchcock
[REVEAL] MS: cross-cut to cursed person preparing trap, audience knows, character doesn't, Hitchcock
CU: knight face, dolly zoom, reality distorts as curse awareness grows, Hitchcock-Vertigo
grade: cold blue+sole warm candle, Hitchcock-grade, Kodak 500T
style: Hitchcock suspense, information gap, POV subjective, dolly zoom = curse warps space
`

### 7.2 Johnson式密室(城堡谋杀)

`
WS: grand hall, sealed doors, each suspect in different color, Johnson-Knives, 9:16
MS: investigator moves through suspects, each prop is evidence, Johnson clue framing
ECU: bloody dagger on table, every object = evidence, prop ECU, Johnson
[FLASHBACK] WS: same hall, different version of events, multiple perspectives, Johnson
grade: warm autumn tones, each character one color, Johnson-grade, Kodak 250D
style: Johnson sealed space, every frame = evidence, color = faction, multiple perspectives
`

### 7.3 Lynch式梦境恐惧(诅咒小镇)

`
WS: village square, normal, warm light, cracks in reality, Lynch-dream, 9:16
MS: figure walks, something wrong in background, slow push-in discovering anomaly, Lynch
CU: face, smile held too long, normal becomes horror, Lynch
[SUDDEN] WS: red curtain space, dream logic, reality fractures, Lynch-TwinPeaks
grade: suburban warm → red+green+black shift, Lynch palette, reality fractures
style: Lynch dream logic, cracks in mundane, red curtain = subconscious, normal becomes uncanny
`
