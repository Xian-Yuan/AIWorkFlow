## 1. David Lean — 史诗的壮游(Lawrence of Arabia/River Kwai)

### 1.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 大升降+航拍+精确轨道+缓慢横移 | grand crane, aerial, precision track, slow lateral, Lean-style |
| 构图 | 深焦超宽(2.35:1)+沙漠/海洋占大比例+人在1/4=史诗即空间 | deep focus ultra-wide, desert/sea dominant, human at quarter, epic as space, Lean-grade |
| 调色 | 沙漠黄+天空蓝+日落金=史诗即光线 | desert yellow, sky blue, sunset gold, epic as light, Lean-palette |
| 光线 | 自然光+逆光+沙漠硬光+黄金时段=史诗即黄金 | natural, backlight, desert hard, golden hour, epic as gold, Lean-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 大建立→中近→特写→大建立=节奏来自尺度 | grand establish -> medium -> close -> grand establish, rhythm from scale, Lean-editing |
| 叙事 | 个人vs帝国+文明vs荒漠+桥梁即文明=尺度即叙事 | individual vs empire, civilization vs desert, bridge as civilization, scale as narrative, Lean-narrative |

### 1.2 拉片精华

**Lawrence of Arabia (1962)**: 沙漠从左到右横移+人影=沙漠即主角; 沙暴=自然即上帝; 两次进入沙漠(第一次好奇/第二次崩溃)=沙漠即心理
AI适配: `desert lateral, human shadow, desert as protagonist, sandstorm as God, two desert entries, Lean-Lawrence`

**Bridge on the River Kwai (1957)**: 桥梁+热带+战争=建设即疯狂; 桥=文明/炸桥=回归荒野
AI适配: `bridge, tropical, war, building as madness, bridge as civilization, Lean-Kwai`

**Doctor Zhivago (1965)**: 雪+革命+爱情=革命即冰; 冰雪覆盖一切=政治即冻结
AI适配: `snow, revolution, love, revolution as ice, politics as freeze, Lean-Zhivago`

### 1.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Lean适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|---------|--------|
| 大沙漠史诗 | 深焦超宽+沙漠占大比例+黄金时段 | deep focus ultra-wide, desert dominant, golden hour, epic as space, Lean-style |
| 桥梁/城堡建设 | 桥梁+热带/森林+建设即疯狂 | bridge, forest, building as madness, Lean-Kwai |
| 雪地/冬天战争 | 雪+革命+冻结 | snow, revolution, politics as freeze, Lean-Zhivago |

## 2. Orson Welles — 深焦的先驱(Citizen Kane)

### 2.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 深焦+升降+快速横移+急推 | deep focus, crane, fast lateral, crash zoom, Welles-style |
| 构图 | 深焦三区同时叙事+天花板可见+低角度仰拍=权力即天花板 | deep focus three-zone, ceiling visible, low angle仰, power as ceiling, Welles-grade |
| 调色 | 黑白+高对比+灰阶丰富=真相即灰度 | B&W, high contrast, rich grey, truth as greyscale, Welles-palette |
| 光线 | 深焦+前景清晰+后景清晰=一切同时可见=真相即全景 | deep focus, foreground sharp, background sharp, all visible, truth as panorama, Welles-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 跳切(时间压缩)+蒙太奇+闪电剪辑=时间即碎片 | jump cut, montage, lightning cut, time as fragments, Welles-editing |
| 叙事 | 真相不可知+多视角+谁是Kane?=真相即拼图 | truth unknowable, multi-perspective, who is Kane?, truth as puzzle, Welles-narrative |

### 2.2 拍片精华

**Citizen Kane (1941)**: 深焦+天花板+低角度=权力即建筑; 闪回拼图=真相即拼图; "Rosebud"=一个词即一个人生
AI适配: `deep focus, ceiling visible, low angle仰, power as architecture, flashback puzzle, Welles-CitizenKane`

**Touch of Evil (1958)**: 开场3分钟长镜头+深焦+横移=罪恶即空间
AI适配: `3-minute opening long take, deep focus, lateral, evil as space, Welles-TouchOfEvil`

**The Magnificent Ambersons (1942)**: 时间流逝+社会变化=旧世界即新世界吞噬
AI适配: `time passing, social change, old world swallowed by new, Welles-Ambersons`

### 2.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Welles适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|----------|--------|
| 领主的权力全景 | 深焦三区+天花板+低角度 | deep focus three-zone, ceiling visible, low angle, power as architecture, Welles-style |
| 真相拼图 | 多视角+闪回+真相不可知 | multi-perspective, flashback, truth as puzzle, Welles-CitizenKane |
| 犯罪空间 | 长镜头建立+深焦+横移 | long take establish, deep focus, lateral, evil as space, Welles-TouchOfEvil |

## 3. Sam Mendes — 一镜到底的战争(1917)

### 3.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 一镜到底+手持+稳定器+环绕+不切 | one-take illusion, handheld, Steadicam, orbit, no cuts, Mendes-1917-style |
| 构图 | 跟随士兵+战场纵深+废墟+黄昏=战争即旅程 | follow soldier, battlefield depth, ruins, dusk, war as journey, Mendes-1917-grade |
| 调色 | 去饱和+冷蓝+泥土+唯一暖=火光=战争即泥土 | desaturated, cold blue, mud, only warm is firelight, war as mud, Mendes-1917-palette |
| 光线 | 自然光+战壕暗+夜间火光+破墙射入=战争即光线断裂 | natural, trench dark, night firelight, light through broken wall, war as fractured light, Mendes-1917-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 伪一镜到底(隐藏剪辑点)+时间连续=战争即不中断 | fake one-take, hidden cuts, time continuous, war as uninterrupted, Mendes-1917-editing |
| 叙事 | 任务即旅程+兄弟即责任+时间即生死 | mission as journey, brother as duty, time as life/death, Mendes-1917-narrative |

### 3.2 拉片精华

**1917 (2019)**: 一镜到底+战壕+废墟+黄昏+河=战争即不中断的恐惧; 跑过战场=观众即士兵; 火光照亮废墟=战争中唯一温暖
AI适配: `one-take, trench, ruins, dusk, river, war as uninterrupted fear, run through battlefield, firelight on ruins, Mendes-1917`

**Road to Perdition (2002)**: 雨+黑帮+父子+雨中枪战=父亲即保护; 50mm+雨+深景深=犯罪即雨
AI适配: `rain, gangster, father-son, rain gunfight, father as protection, 50mm, Mendes-Perdition`

**Skyfall (2012)**: 伦敦+地铁+岛屿+火力=Bond即建筑; 大升降+航拍+IMAX=谍战即史诗
AI适配: `London, subway, island,火力, Bond as architecture, grand crane aerial, Mendes-Skyfall`

| 一镜到底战场 | 伪一镜到底+战壕+废墟+火光 | one-take illusion, trench, ruins, firelight, Mendes-1917-style |

## 4. Spike Jonze + Charlie Kaufman — 元叙事的迷宫

### 4.1 签名美学(Jonze导演 + Kaufman编剧)

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 手持+缓慢+漂移+主观变形+迷你化 | handheld, slow, drift, subjective distortion, miniaturization, Jonze-Kaufman-style |
| 构图 | 建筑/人缩小(Being John Malkovich)+双重世界+框架=意识即建筑 | shrinking, dual world, framed, consciousness as architecture, Jonze-Kaufman-grade |
| 调色 | 柔暖(Her)+冷灰(Adaptation)+褪色=情绪即褪色 | soft warm Her, cold grey Adaptation, faded, emotion as fading, Jonze-Kaufman-palette |
| 光线 | 自然柔光+窗光+屏幕光=连接即光线 | natural soft, window, screen light, connection as light, Jonze-Kaufman-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 匹配剪辑(身体→意识)+缩小→放大=意识即剪辑 | match cut body to mind, shrink to enlarge, consciousness as editing, Jonze-Kaufman-editing |
| 叙事 | 元叙事=故事即故事+身份即虚构+AI即镜像(Her)=创作即自我解构 | meta-narrative, story about story, identity as fiction, AI as mirror, creation as self-deconstruction, Kaufman-narrative |

### 4.2 拉片精华

**Her (2013)**: 柔暖+窗光+屏幕光+AI声音=爱即距离; 高腰裤+未来洛杉矶=未来即过去
AI适配: `soft warm, window light, screen glow, AI voice, love as distance, high-waist future LA, future as past, Jonze-Her`

**Being John Malkovich (1999)**: 缩小+7楼半+意识入口=意识即建筑; 进入他人=身份即偷窥
AI适配: `miniaturization, floor 7.5, consciousness portal, consciousness as architecture, enter another, Jonze-Malkovich`

**Adaptation (2002)**: 双重叙事(剧本/现实)+ Kaufman写自己=创作即噩梦; 热+沼泽=创作即腐烂
AI适配: `dual narrative script/reality, writer writing himself, creation as nightmare, heat swamp, Kaufman-Adaptation`

### 4.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Jonze-Kaufman适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|-----------------|--------|
| 魔法意识入口 | 缩小+意识即建筑+进入他人 | miniaturization, consciousness as architecture, enter another mind, Jonze-Malkovich |
| AI/魔法镜像 | 柔暖+窗光+屏幕光+爱即距离 | soft warm, window, screen, love as distance, Jonze-Her |
| 元叙事/创作即自我 | 双重叙事+创作即噩梦 | dual narrative, creation as nightmare, Kaufman-Adaptation |

## 5. Apichatpong Weerasethakul — 东南亚的梦(Uncle Boonmee)

### 5.1 签名美学

| 维度 | 特征 | 英文提示词 |
|------|------|-----------|
| 运镜 | 锁定+极慢+漂浮+长镜头+不手持 | locked-off, extremely slow, floating, long take, no handheld, Apichatpong-style |
| 构图 | 自然+树林+水+动物+鬼魂+人在边缘 | nature, forest, water, animal, ghost, human at edge, Apichatpong-grade |
| 调色 | 自然饱和+绿+蓝+暖+月=梦即自然 | natural saturation, green, blue, warm, moon, dream as nature, Apichatpong-palette |
| 光线 | 自然光+月光+萤火虫+火=梦即光 | natural, moonlight, fireflies, fire, dream as light, Apichatpong-lighting |
| 剪辑 | 极少+长镜头+时间流逝+不切=梦即时间 | minimal cuts, long takes, time passes, no cuts, dream as time, Apichatpong-editing |
| 叙事 | 梦即现实+鬼魂即日常+动物即亲属+东南亚信仰=死后即另一个世界 | dream as reality, ghost as everyday, animal as relative, Southeast Asian belief, afterlife as other world, Apichatpong-narrative |

### 5.2 拉片精华

**Uncle Boonmee Who Can Recall His Past Lives (2010)**: 长镜头+鬼出现+猩猩+水=死亡即日常; 鬼不恐怖=死亡即回家
AI适配: `long take, ghost appears, ape, water, death as everyday, ghost not scary, death as homecoming, Apichatpong-Boonmee`

**Tropical Malady (2004)**: 前半城市+后半丛林=文明即野兽; 转折不解释=两个世界即一个世界
AI适配: `first half city, second half jungle, civilization as beast, no explanation, two worlds one world, Apichatpong-Tropical`

### 5.3 AI短剧适配

| 西幻场景 | Apichatpong适配 | 提示词 |
|----------|----------------|--------|
| 魔法森林/鬼魂日常 | 锁定+长镜头+鬼不恐怖+月 | locked-off, long take, ghost not scary, moonlight, Apichatpong-style |
| 人vs兽/文明vs荒野 | 前半城市→后半丛林 | first half city second half jungle, civilization as beast, Apichatpong-Tropical |
