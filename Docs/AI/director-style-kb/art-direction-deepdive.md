## 2. 世界构建方法论(5种)

### 2.1 考据式世界构建(Eggers/Carter)

| 原则 | 方法 | 检查清单 | AI提示词 |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| 真实先行 | 先研究历史/再创造 | 每个细节有史料出处 | period-accurate, historically researched, no anachronism, authentic material culture, Eggers-method |
| 材质真实 | 只用该时代有的材料 | 木材/铁/蜡/麻/石 | uthentic materials only, iron not steel, wax not electric, hemp not nylon, stone not concrete |
| 光源逻辑 | 只用该时代有的光源 | 烛/火把/壁炉/日光 | period light sources only, candle/torch/fireplace/sunlight, no anachronistic light |
| 空间逻辑 | 建筑符合该时代技术 | 石砌/木构/夯土 | period architecture, stone masonry/timber frame, era-appropriate construction techniques |

### 2.2 风格化世界构建(Anderson/Gassner)

| 原则 | 方法 | 检查清单 | AI提示词 |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| 色彩先行 | 先定调色板/再设计 | 每个场景=3色以内 | stylized palette, limited 3 colors per scene, Anderson color control, designed not natural |
| 对称强迫 | 一切居中/一切对称 | 偏离对称=有原因 | strict symmetry, centered composition, deviation from center is intentional, Anderson-order |
| 平面化 | 减少纵深/正面拍摄 | 深度=混乱 | lattened depth, frontal staging, minimal z-axis, plane not volume, Anderson-flat |
| 物件叙事 | 每个物件=精确放置 | 无随机物件 | every object intentional, precise placement, no random elements, curated world, Anderson-curation |

### 2.3 生物式世界构建(del Toro/Caballero)

| 原则 | 方法 | 检查清单 | AI提示词 |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| 有机体 | 建筑/物件/服装都是活的 | 石头有血管/墙会呼吸 | organic architecture, living walls, breathing stone, veins in columns, del Toro-bio |
| 腐败美 | 美=有生命=在腐败 | 新=假/旧=真 | eautiful decay, patina as truth, aging as beauty, lived-in world, del Toro-patina |
| 湿润感 | 一切有点潮/有点湿 | 干=死/湿=活 | damp atmosphere, slight moisture everywhere, humidity of life, del Toro-wet |
| 双重逻辑 | 两个世界各有完整规则 | 魔法世界不是现实+魔法 | dual logic systems, fantasy world has own physics, not reality+magic but separate reality, del Toro-dual |

### 2.4 尺度式世界构建(Nolan/Crowley/Villeneuve)

| 原则 | 方法 | 检查清单 | AI提示词 |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| 人体尺度 | 人5%的帧=渺小 | 每个建立镜头验证人体比 | human 5% of frame, overwhelming architecture, Nolan-scale, environment dominant |
| IMAX尺度 | 最宽+最深+最高 | 每个揭示用IMAX | IMAX scale, widest+deepest+highest, maximum information per frame, Nolan-IMAX |
| 真实建造 | 建真的/不是CG | 能建就建 | 
eal construction, built set not CG, physical reality, Crowley-build |
| 天气元素 | 沙/雪/雨/雾=叙事工具 | 每个大场面有天气 | weather as narrative, sand/snow/rain/fog=emotion, elemental storytelling, Villeneuve-weather |

### 2.5 文化式世界构建(Beachler/Carter)

| 原则 | 方法 | 检查清单 | AI提示词 |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| 文化特异 | 不用西欧默认 | 每个文化有自己的逻辑 | culturally specific, non-Western default, each culture has own architecture logic, Beachler-culture |
| 色彩编码 | 色彩=文化=身份 | 家族色/阶级色/魔法色 | cultural color coding, family color, class color, magic color, identity through palette, Beachler-coding |
| 第三路径 | 传统+现代=第三种 | 不是A或B/是C | 	hird path, tradition+futurism=new form, not A or B but C, Beachler-synthesis |

## 3. 场景设计原则(10条)

| # | 原则 | 解释 | 提示词 |
|---|------|------|--------|
| 1 | 场景即角色 | 空间有性格/有历史/有意图 | space as character, room has personality, architecture has intention, set breathes |
| 2 | 光源必须合理 | 每束光有来源/每个暗有原因 | motivated light, every beam has source, every shadow has reason, no unmotivated illumination |
| 3 | 物件即叙事 | 每个物件讲故事/无随机 | every object tells story, no random props, curated detail, narrative objects |
| 4 | 门=选择 | 每道门=可能的未来 | doorway as choice, threshold as decision, what lies beyond, passage as narrative |
| 5 | 窗=内外世界 | 窗分隔两种现实 | window as divide, inside vs outside, protection vs exposure, glass as boundary |
| 6 | 楼梯=阶级/变化 | 上=权力/下=危险 | stairs as hierarchy, up=power, down=danger, vertical movement=class movement |
| 7 | 走廊=命运 | 一点透视=不可逃避 | corridor as fate, one-point perspective, inescapable path, tunnel vision |
| 8 | 天花板=压迫 | 低=窒息/高=崇高 | ceiling as pressure, low=suffocation, high=sublime, vertical space=psychological space |
| 9 | 地面=现实 | 泥=底层/石=秩序/金=权力 | loor as reality, mud=underclass, stone=order, gold=power, ground truth |
| 10 | 墙=边界 | 墙=保护/也是牢笼 | wall as boundary, protection and prison, enclosure has two sides, wall duality |
